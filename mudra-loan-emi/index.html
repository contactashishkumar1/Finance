PMMY Mudra Loan Categories (2026)

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) provides micro-business credit without requiring security collateral:

Category Loan Amount Target Business Stage
Shishu Up to ₹50,000 Startups & micro vendors
Kishore ₹50,001 to ₹5 Lakhs Growing small businesses
Tarun ₹5 Lakhs to ₹10 Lakhs Established business expansion

PMMY Mudra Loan Categories (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun)

  • Shishu: Collateral-free micro loans up to ₹50,000 for small vendors and startups.
  • Kishore: Business expansion loans from ₹50,000 up to ₹5 Lakhs.
  • Tarun: Scale-up business loans from ₹5 Lakhs up to ₹10 Lakhs (and expanded ₹20L under 2026 scheme).

No Collateral Security Requirement

  • CGTMSE Coverage: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana loans are guaranteed by CGTMSE credit protection. Banks cannot demand third-party collateral or land assets.
  • Repayment Tenure: Flexible repayment terms ranging from 3 to 5 years with nominal processing fees.

Mudra Loan Application Tip

Maintain a clean Udyam Registration Certificate and 12-month GST filing record to speed up Mudra loan approval at PSU bank branches.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the 3 categories of Mudra Loans?
1. Shishu: Loans up to ₹50,000. 2. Kishore: Loans from ₹50,001 to ₹5 Lakhs. 3. Tarun: Loans from ₹5 Lakhs to ₹10 Lakhs (extended up to ₹20 Lakhs for eligible units).
Is collateral required for a Mudra Loan?
No, Mudra loans under PMMY are collateral-free micro-business loans backed by Government credit guarantee.
What is the interest rate for Mudra loans in 2026?
Mudra loan interest rates depend on the lending bank, typically ranging between 8.5% and 12% p.a.
What is the maximum repayment tenure for Mudra loans?
Mudra loan repayment tenures range from 3 to 5 years (up to 60 months).
Who is eligible for a Mudra Loan?
Non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises, shopkeepers, artisans, fruit vendors, and small manufacturers in India.
Is there a processing fee on Shishu Mudra loans?
No, banks do not charge any processing fee for Shishu category Mudra loans (up to ₹50,000).
How is Mudra Loan EMI calculated?
EMI = P * r * (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is Loan Principal, r is Monthly Interest Rate, and n is Tenure in months.
Can women entrepreneurs get concessions on Mudra Loans?
Yes, many public sector banks offer a 0.25% interest rate rebate for women entrepreneurs under PMMY.
