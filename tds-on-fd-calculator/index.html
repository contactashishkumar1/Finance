Section 194A TDS Regulations (2026)

Banks in India are mandated under Section 194A of the Income Tax Act to deduct tax at source when annual FD interest exceeds designated thresholds.

Taxpayer Category Annual Interest Threshold TDS Rate (with PAN) TDS Rate (without PAN)
Individual (< 60 Yrs) ₹40,000 10% 20% (Sec 206AA)
Senior Citizen (60+ Yrs) ₹50,000 10% 20% (Sec 206AA)

Section 194A TDS Threshold Limits & Rules (2026)

  • Regular Taxpayers (< 60 Yrs): Bank TDS triggers when annual FD interest across branches exceeds ₹40,000.
  • Senior Citizens (60+ Yrs): Bank TDS triggers when annual FD interest exceeds ₹50,000.
  • TDS Rates: 10% TDS with PAN card; 20% TDS under Section 206AA if PAN is not submitted.

Preventing TDS via Form 15G and Form 15H

  • Form 15G (Under 60): Submit at start of financial year if total estimated taxable income is below basic exemption limit.
  • Form 15H (Senior Citizens): Submit to prevent TDS if net tax liability is zero.
  • Section 80TTB Relief: Senior citizens can claim up to ₹50,000 interest deduction on FD/Savings interest income.

FD Tax Refund Hack

If the bank deducted TDS but your overall annual income is non-taxable, file your ITR to claim a 100% direct refund of the deducted TDS with interest!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the TDS threshold limit on FD interest under Section 194A?
For regular taxpayers, bank TDS is triggered when total annual FD interest across branches of a bank exceeds ₹40,000. For senior citizens (60+ years), the threshold is ₹50,000.
What is the standard TDS rate on bank FD interest?
The standard TDS rate is 10% if PAN card details are updated with the bank. If PAN is not provided, TDS is deducted at 20% under Section 206AA.
What is Form 15G and Form 15H?
Form 15G (for individuals under 60) and Form 15H (for senior citizens 60+) are self-declaration forms submitted to banks to prevent TDS deduction if total estimated taxable income is zero.
Does submitting Form 15G/15H mean FD interest is tax-free?
No. Form 15G/15H only prevents upfront TDS deduction at source. FD interest is fully taxable at your applicable income tax slab rate.
Is TDS deducted on 5-Year Tax Saving FDs?
Yes, interest earned on 5-year tax-saving FDs is subject to Section 194A TDS if annual interest exceeds ₹40,000 (or ₹50,000 for senior citizens).
How to claim refund of excess TDS deducted on FD interest?
If excess TDS was deducted, file your annual Income Tax Return (ITR) to claim a direct refund into your verified bank account.
What is Section 80TTB deduction for Senior Citizens?
Section 80TTB allows senior citizens to claim a tax deduction of up to ₹50,000 on total interest income earned from savings accounts and bank FDs.
Are Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) subject to the same TDS limits?
No. For corporate FDs with NBFCs, the Section 194A TDS threshold is lower at ₹5,000 per financial year.
