  • Tax Rebate: Stamp duty and registration fees paid during the financial year can be claimed as a tax deduction under Income Tax Section 80C up to ₹1.5 Lakhs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stamp Duty in property registration?
Stamp Duty is a state government tax levied on property sale agreements to grant legal ownership and validity under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899.
Are stamp duty rates different for female buyers in India?
Yes, several Indian states (such as Delhi, UP, Haryana, and Punjab) offer a 1% to 2% rebate on stamp duty rates for property registered solely or jointly in the name of a woman.
What is the standard registration fee cap in Maharashtra?
In Maharashtra, property registration fee is 1% of the agreement value, capped at a maximum of ₹30,000 for properties priced above ₹30 Lakhs.
Is stamp duty calculated on Agreement Value or Circle Rate?
Stamp duty is calculated on whichever figure is HIGHER: the agreed property purchase price (Agreement Value) or the official state Circle Rate (Ready Reckoner Rate).
Can stamp duty be claimed as a tax deduction under Section 80C?
Yes, stamp duty and property registration fees paid during the financial year can be claimed as a deduction under Income Tax Section 80C up to a maximum cap of ₹1,50,000.
What is Metro Cess in stamp duty calculation?
Metro Cess is an additional 1% surcharge levied by state governments (such as Maharashtra and Karnataka) in municipal corporation areas to fund urban transit infrastructure.
What happens if stamp duty is unpaid or underpaid?
Property documents with unpaid or underpaid stamp duty are inadmissible as legal evidence in court, and state governments levy penalties ranging from 2% to 200% per month on unpaid stamp fees.
Is home loan available to fund stamp duty costs?
RBI regulations prohibit banks from including stamp duty and registration fees in the Sanctioned Loan-to-Value (LTV) calculation. Homebuyers must fund stamp duty out of their own pocket.
