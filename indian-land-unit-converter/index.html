Indian Land Measurement Units Guide

State-wise Land Unit Variations

Unlike international metric units, land area measurement in India varies significantly by state. While 1 Gaj (9 sq ft) and 1 Acre (43,560 sq ft) are standard, units like Bigha and Biswa have different square feet values in UP, Bengal, Bihar, and Punjab.

Property Rate Valuation Formula

Total Land Price = Land Area Quantity × Rate per Unit.

To compare deals across different cities, always convert land price into Rate per Square Foot (Total Valuation / Total Sq Ft).

💡 Gaj to Sq Ft Hack

1 Gaj is exactly 9 Square Feet (1 Sq Yard = 3ft × 3ft = 9 sq ft). Multiply Gaj quantity by 9 to get exact square footage instantly.

⚡ Guntha in South & West

1 Guntha equals 1,089 sq ft (33ft × 33ft). 40 Gunthas make exactly 1 Acre in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Gujarat.

🛡️ Verify Circle Rate

Before buying land, check the government circle rate (ready reckoner rate) per sq ft to calculate minimum stamp duty payable during registration.

⚠️ 3 Common Land Unit Conversion Mistakes

  • • Assuming Bigha is Uniform Across India: 1 Bigha in UP is 27,225 sq ft, while 1 Bigha in Bengal is 14,400 sq ft. Always verify state-specific definitions.
  • • Confusing Square Feet with Linear Feet: Land area is measured in two-dimensional square feet (length × width), not linear running feet.
  • • Ignoring Road Cut & Setback Area: Total plot area purchased differs from net buildable area after deducting mandatory municipal road setbacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Square Feet is 1 Bigha in India?
1 Bigha varies by state. In Rajasthan and UP, 1 Bigha = 27,225 sq ft (Pucca Bigha). In Bihar and WB, 1 Bigha = 14,400 sq ft. In Punjab/Haryana, 1 Bigha = 9,075 sq ft.
How many Square Feet is 1 Gaj?
1 Gaj is equal to 9 Square Feet (or 1 Square Yard). Gaj is widely used in Delhi NCR, Haryana, Punjab, and Uttar Pradesh for plotting residential land.
How many Square Feet is 1 Guntha?
1 Guntha is equal to 1,089 Square Feet (or 121 Square Yards). Guntha is widely used in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.
How many Square Feet is 1 Ground in Tamil Nadu?
1 Ground is equal to 2,400 Square Feet. It is the standard land measurement unit used for real estate plots in Chennai and Tamil Nadu.
How many Square Feet is 1 Acre?
1 Acre is universally equal to 43,560 Square Feet (or 4,840 Square Yards / 40.47 Ares / 0.4047 Hectares).
How many Square Feet is 1 Kanal?
1 Kanal is equal to 5,445 Square Feet (or 20 Marlas / 605 Square Yards). It is predominantly used in Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, and Jammu & Kashmir.
How is total land property price calculated?
Total Land Price = Land Area Quantity × Rate per Unit. For example, 500 Gaj @ ₹25,000 per Gaj = ₹1,25,00,000 (₹1.25 Crore).
What is the difference between Pucca Bigha and Kacha Bigha?
Pucca Bigha (27,225 sq ft) is the standard official measurement. Kacha Bigha (often 1/3rd of Pucca Bigha = 9,075 sq ft) is used informally in rural Western UP and parts of Rajasthan.
