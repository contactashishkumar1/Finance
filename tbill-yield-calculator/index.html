RBI Treasury Bill (T-Bill) Mechanics & Tenures

  • Zero-Coupon Discount Bonds: T-Bills pay no periodic interest coupons. Issued at a discount to Par (₹100) and redeemed at full ₹100 Face Value at maturity.
  • 3 Tenures: 91-Day T-Bills (weekly auction), 182-Day T-Bills, and 364-Day T-Bills (bi-weekly auctions).

Annualized YTM Formula & Tax Treatment

  • YTM Formula: Annualized Yield % = [(100 - Cutoff Price) / Cutoff Price] * (365 / Tenure Days) * 100.
  • Zero TDS & STCG Tax: Zero TDS deducted at source. Returns are taxed as Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) at your income tax slab rate.
  • Retail Direct Buying: Retail investors can buy T-Bills directly via RBI Retail Direct Portal or Zerodha/Groww apps.

Sovereign Safety Advantage

Treasury Bills offer 100% sovereign credit backing from the Government of India, making them safer than bank FDs for parking short-term surplus funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Treasury Bills (T-Bills)?
Treasury Bills (T-Bills) are short-term money market debt instruments issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on behalf of the Government of India.
What are the standard T-Bill tenures in India?
RBI issues T-Bills in three maturity tenures: 91-Day T-Bills, 182-Day T-Bills, and 364-Day T-Bills.
How do T-Bills generate returns without paying coupons?
T-Bills are zero-coupon securities issued at a discount to Face Value (₹100) and redeemed at full Face Value (₹100) upon maturity.
What is the annualized yield formula for T-Bills?
Formula: Annualized Yield % = [(Face Value - Purchase Price) / Purchase Price] * (365 / Tenure Days) * 100.
How is T-Bill interest income taxed in India?
T-Bill returns are treated as Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) and added to the investor's total income, taxed at their applicable slab rate.
Is TDS deducted on RBI Treasury Bills?
No. There is zero Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on RBI Treasury Bills.
How can retail investors buy T-Bills in India?
Retail investors can purchase T-Bills directly through the RBI Retail Direct Portal or via stockbrokers (Zerodha Coin, Groww) in non-competitive bidding auctions.
Are T-Bills safer than Bank Fixed Deposits?
Yes. T-Bills carry sovereign credit backing from the Government of India, making them risk-free short-term investments superior to bank deposits.
