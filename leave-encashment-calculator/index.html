Section 10(10AA) Leave Encashment Rules (2026)

Leave encashment is the payment received by an employee for unavailed accumulated earned leaves at the time of retirement or resignation.

Category Timing of Encashment Tax Exemption Ceiling
Government Employees At Retirement / Resignation 100% Tax-Free (No Limit)
Private Sector Employees At Retirement / Resignation Min of 4 Factors (Max ₹25 Lakhs)
All Employees During Service / Employment 100% Taxable (Added to Salary)

Section 10(10AA) Leave Encashment Exemption Formula

  • 4-Factor Exemption Test: Minimum of (1) Actual Encashment Received, (2) ₹25,00,000 Statutory Cap, (3) 10 Months Average Basic+DA Salary, (4) Cash equivalent of unavailed earned leave (max 30 days/yr limit).
  • ₹25 Lakh Cap Expansion: Finance Act increased the non-government private employee exemption limit from ₹3 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs.
  • Government Employees: Central/State Govt employees get 100% tax-free leave encashment at retirement.

Encashment During Service vs At Retirement

  • During Employment: Leave encashment received while continuing employment is 100% fully taxable under "Income from Salary".
  • At Retirement / Resignation: Exemption benefits under Section 10(10AA) apply only upon retirement, resignation, or superannuation.

Leave Encashment Pro Tip

Avoid encashing leaves during active service. Accumulate leaves up to your company limit and encash them at resignation to claim the ₹25 Lakh tax-free exemption!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the statutory tax exemption limit for Leave Encashment in 2026?
Under Section 10(10AA), the maximum lifetime tax exemption cap for non-government private sector employees is ₹25 Lakhs (increased from ₹3 Lakhs by the Govt).
Is Leave Encashment received by Government employees tax-free?
Yes, leave encashment received by Central or State Government employees at retirement or resignation is 100% exempt from income tax without any limit.
Is leave encashment received DURING employment taxable?
Yes! Leave encashment received while continuing service is 100% taxable as salary income for both private and government employees.
How is Section 10(10AA) exemption calculated for private employees?
Exemption is the minimum of 4 amounts: 1. Actual Leave Encashment Received, 2. ₹25 Lakh statutory cap, 3. 10 months' average salary, 4. Salary for unavailed leave (max 30 days leave entitlement per year of completed service).
What salary components are considered for leave encashment?
Salary for Section 10(10AA) includes average Basic Salary and Dearness Allowance (DA) for the 10 months preceding retirement.
What happens if company policy allows more than 30 days leave per year?
For income tax exemption purposes under Section 10(10AA), maximum leave entitlement per year is capped at 30 days, even if company policy grants 40 or 45 days.
Is leave encashment taxable under the New Tax Regime?
Yes, Section 10(10AA) leave encashment exemption up to ₹25 Lakhs is fully available under both Old and New Tax Regimes.
What happens to leave encashment in case of an employee's death?
Leave encashment paid to the legal heirs or family members of a deceased employee is completely exempt from income tax.
