What was the Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)?

PMVVY was a pension scheme announced by the Government of India exclusively for senior citizens aged 60 years and above. Operated by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), it provided an assured return (pension) for a policy term of 10 years.

Key Features for Existing Subscribers

  • Guaranteed Return: For policies purchased in the final year (FY 2022-23), the scheme offered an assured pension rate of 7.4% p.a. payable monthly.
  • Tenure: The policy term is exactly 10 years.
  • Maturity Benefit: If the pensioner survives to the end of the 10-year term, the Purchase Price (initial investment) along with the final pension installment is refunded.
  • Death Benefit: Upon the death of the pensioner during the 10-year term, the entire Purchase Price is refunded to the nominee or legal heirs.

Taxability of PMVVY

It is important for existing subscribers to remember their tax liabilities:

  • The regular pension amounts you receive (monthly, quarterly, etc.) are not tax-free. They are fully taxable according to your income tax slab.
  • However, the scheme was exempt from GST.

Loan Facility

A highly useful feature for existing subscribers is the loan facility. Once you have completed 3 policy years, you can avail of a loan against your PMVVY policy to meet emergency medical needs. The maximum loan amount that can be granted is 75% of your original purchase price. The loan interest will be recovered from your future pension installments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PMVVY still open for new investments in 2026?
No, the Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY) was officially closed for new subscriptions on March 31, 2023. This calculator is provided for existing subscribers to track their pensions.
What was the interest rate for PMVVY?
For the final financial year before its closure (FY 2022-23), the scheme offered a guaranteed pension rate of 7.4% per annum payable monthly.
What is the tenure of PMVVY?
The policy term for PMVVY is exactly 10 years.
What happens after the 10-year tenure is complete?
At the end of the 10-year policy term, if the pensioner survives, the initial purchase price (principal amount) along with the final pension installment is refunded to them.
Is the pension from PMVVY taxable?
Yes, the pension received under PMVVY is fully taxable. It must be declared under the 'Income from Other Sources' head while filing your income tax returns.
What was the maximum investment limit for PMVVY?
A senior citizen could invest a maximum of ₹15,00,000 (15 Lakhs) in the PMVVY scheme, which yielded a maximum monthly pension of ₹9,250.
Can I take a loan against my PMVVY policy?
Yes, existing subscribers can avail a loan against their PMVVY policy after completing 3 policy years. The maximum loan amount granted is 75% of the initial purchase price.
What happens in case of the pensioner's death during the 10 years?
If the pensioner dies during the 10-year term, the initial purchase price (principal) is refunded to the designated nominee or legal heirs.
