Pivot Point Formulas & Intraday Trading Guide

Standard (Floor) Pivot

P = (High + Low + Close) / 3

R1 = 2P - Low | S1 = 2P - High

R2 = P + (High - Low) | S2 = P - (High - Low)

Fibonacci Pivot

P = (High + Low + Close) / 3

R1 = P + 0.382 × (High - Low)

R2 = P + 0.618 × (High - Low)

💡 Pro Hack: Narrow CPR Breakout

When the distance between Top Central (TC) and Bottom Central (BC) pivot is extremely narrow, it signals an explosive trending day in Nifty or BankNifty. Look for opening range breakouts above TC or below BC.

⚡ Importance of Camarilla H3/L3

Camarilla H3 and L3 act as strong mean-reversion boundary zones. Buying near L3 or shorting near H3 provides high-probability range-bound intraday setups.

🛡️ Institutional Alignment

Unlike lagging moving averages, pivot points are calculated before the market opens. Institutional algorithmic execution algorithms use these exact levels to place block orders.

⚠️ 3 Common Pitfalls in Pivot Point Trading

  • • Trading Pivots in Isolation: Never buy at S1 or sell at R1 without confirming volume, candlestick patterns, or broader market trend direction.
  • • Placing Stop Losses Right at Pivot Lines: Market makers often sweep liquidity right beyond pivot levels. Place stop losses slightly beyond the level.
  • • Using Weekly Pivots for 1-Minute Scalping: Match your pivot timeframe with your trading horizon. Use daily pivots for intraday 5-min charts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Pivot Point in Stock Trading?
A pivot point is a technical indicator calculated from the high, low, and closing prices of the previous trading day. It represents the primary directional bias and key support and resistance levels for the current trading day.
How is the Standard Pivot Point calculated?
Pivot Point (P) = (High + Low + Close) / 3. R1 = (2 × P) - Low. S1 = (2 × P) - High. R2 = P + (High - Low). S2 = P - (High - Low).
What is the Camarilla Pivot Point method?
Camarilla pivot points use a set of 8 levels (H1-H4, L1-L4) based on yesterday's range. Traders use H3/L3 for mean-reversal trades and H4/L4 for breakout trades.
What is the Fibonacci Pivot Point method?
Fibonacci pivot points calculate the central pivot (P = (H+L+C)/3) and apply Fibonacci ratios (38.2%, 61.8%, 100%) to the previous day's price range to establish support and resistance levels.
How to trade using Pivot Points in Nifty & BankNifty?
If price opens above the Central Pivot (P), the intraday bias is bullish (targets R1, R2). If price opens below P, the bias is bearish (targets S1, S2).
Which timeframe is best for Pivot Points?
Daily pivot points are best used on 5-minute, 15-minute, or 1-hour intraday price charts.
What is CPR (Central Pivot Range)?
CPR consists of 3 levels: TC (Top Central = (P - BC) + P), P (Pivot = (H+L+C)/3), and BC (Bottom Central = (H+L)/2). A narrow CPR indicates a high probability of a trending day.
Can I use today's open price in Woodie Pivot calculation?
Yes! The Woodie pivot formula gives extra weight to the open price: Pivot (P) = (High + Low + 2 × Open) / 4.
