Material Optimization & Quality Checks

  • M-Sand vs River Sand: Manufactured Sand (M-Sand) is 30% cheaper than river sand and offers higher compressive strength.
  • AAC Blocks Advantage: AAC blocks reduce structural dead load by 50% and save 15% on mortar cement consumption.

Material Buying Tip

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cement bags are required for 1,000 sq ft house construction?
For a 1,000 sq ft house, approximately 400 bags of cement (50kg bags) are required for foundation, columns, beam slabs, masonry, and plastering.
How much TMT steel is required for 1,000 sq ft house?
Roughly 3.5 to 4 metric tons (3,500 to 4,000 kg) of TMT steel bars (Fe-500D / Fe-550D) are needed for a 1,000 sq ft house RCC structure.
How many bricks are required for a 1,000 sq ft house?
Approximately 18,000 red clay bricks (or around 1,400 AAC blocks) are required for wall masonry in a 1,000 sq ft home.
How much sand and aggregate (coarse aggregate) are needed?
For 1,000 sq ft area: 1,800 cu ft of M-Sand / River Sand and 1,350 cu ft of 20mm/10mm granite aggregates are needed.
What is M-Sand and can it replace river sand?
M-Sand (Manufactured Sand) is crushed granite stone sand. It is fully approved by IS 383 standards and is 20-30% cheaper than river sand.
How much paint is required for a 1,000 sq ft house?
Around 180 to 200 liters of primer and interior/exterior emulsion paint (2 coats) are required.
What tile quantity is required for flooring and bathrooms?
Around 1,300 sq ft of vitrified/ceramic tiles are needed (including 10% wastage allowance for cutting).
What grade of cement should be used for slab RCC?
OPC 53 grade or PPC (Portland Pozzolana Cement) from reputed brands (Ultratech, ACC, Ambuja) is recommended for structural RCC framing.
