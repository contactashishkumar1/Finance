The Hidden Cost of BNPL Late Fees

Why BNPL APRs Skyrocket to 1,000%+

Flat late fees (e.g. ₹250 on a ₹1,500 bill) sound small, but because the loan duration is short (7 days), the annualized percentage rate (APR) is mathematically equivalent to 800% - 1,500% APR!

RBI CIBIL Reporting Mandate

Under RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines, BNPL partners (NBFCs) must report every default to CIBIL. Missing even a ₹500 bill permanently damages your credit score.

💡 Pro Hack: Auto-Debit Setup

Set up UPI Auto-Mandates for your BNPL apps to guarantee 100% on-time repayment and eliminate late fee penalties.

⚡ Request Fee Waiver

If you missed a bill due to a technical glitch, contact customer support immediately. Many BNPL apps issue a one-time late fee waiver for first-time offenders.

🛡️ Limit BNPL Apps to 1

Using multiple BNPL apps (LazyPay + Simpl + Amazon Pay Later) leads to forgotten due dates. Stick to a single platform.

⚠️ 3 Common BNPL Trap Mistakes

  • • Treating BNPL as Free Money: Small micro-transactions add up quickly to large month-end bills.
  • • Ignoring Small Overdue Amounts: Leaving a ₹100 pending balance incurs the full minimum late fee slab (e.g. ₹200).
  • • Deactivating the App Without Settling Dues: Uninstalling the app does NOT clear your debt; lenders will initiate legal collection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later)?
BNPL is a short-term credit service offered by platforms like LazyPay, Simpl, Amazon Pay Later, and Flipkart Pay Later that allows users to make purchases instantly and settle bills after 15 to 30 days.
Why are BNPL late fees so high when expressed as Annualized APR?
BNPL apps charge flat late fees (e.g. ₹250 for missing a ₹1,000 bill by 5 days). Because the tenure is extremely short (5-15 days), a ₹250 fee on ₹1,000 represents an astounding 1,825% Annualized Percentage Rate (APR)!
How is BNPL Late Fee Annualized APR calculated?
Annualized APR % = [(Late Fee + GST) / Outstanding Bill Amount] × (365 / Days Overdue) × 100.
Do BNPL late fees affect CIBIL credit scores in India?
YES! RBI guidelines require all BNPL services to report loans to CIBIL, Experian, and Equifax. Overdue payments beyond 30 days drop credit scores by 50 to 100 points.
Are BNPL late fees subject to 18% GST?
Yes, late payment penalties charged by financial institutions in India attract 18% GST.
How does BNPL compare to Credit Card interest rates?
Credit cards charge 36%-42% APR for revolving debt. BNPL late fees on small amounts routinely exceed 200% to 1,500% APR due to fixed minimum late fee slabs!
What happens if a BNPL bill is unpaid for more than 90 days?
The loan is classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA), recovery agents are assigned, and permanent CIBIL default tags are recorded on your credit history.
How can I avoid BNPL late fee traps?
Enable Auto-Debits from your bank account, set calendar reminders 3 days prior to due date, or clear bills immediately after purchase.
