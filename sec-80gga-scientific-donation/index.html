Section 80GGA Scientific Research Donation Guide

Key Provisions of Section 80GGA

Section 80GGA provides a 100% uncapped tax deduction for contributions made towards scientific research, statistical research, or rural development. It is available to all non-business taxpayers (salaried, house property, capital gains).

Section 80GGA vs Section 80G

Section 80G covers general charitable trusts (often with 50% caps or 10% ATI limits). Section 80GGA specifically covers scientific and rural research funds with an uncapped 100% deduction.

💡 Pro Hack: Always Pay Digitally

Always pay via UPI, NetBanking, or Cheque. Any cash donation above ₹2,000 is 100% disallowed under Section 80GGA.

⚡ No Upper Limit Cap

Unlike Section 80C (₹1.5 Lakh limit), Section 80GGA has no ceiling limit. You can deduct 100% of the donated amount from your taxable income.

🛡️ Verify 80GGA Approval

Ensure the research institution has valid approval under Section 35(1)(ii) / 35CCA and displays it on the donation receipt.

⚠️ 3 Common Section 80GGA Mistakes

  • • Donating in Cash > ₹2,000: Cash payments exceeding ₹2,000 receive ₹0 tax deduction under 80GGA.
  • • Claiming Under New Tax Regime: Section 80GGA is NOT permitted under the New Tax Regime. File under the Old Tax Regime to claim 80GGA.
  • • Claiming by Business Owners: Taxpayers with Business or Professional income must claim research expenses under Section 35, not Section 80GGA.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 80GGA of the Income Tax Act?
Section 80GGA allows a 100% tax deduction on donations made for scientific research, rural development, or statistical research by taxpayers who do NOT have income from business or profession.
Who is eligible to claim Section 80GGA?
Salaried employees, pensioners, and taxpayers earning interest/rental income (having NO business income) can claim Section 80GGA. (Taxpayers with business income claim under Section 35 instead).
What is the cash donation limit under Section 80GGA?
Donations exceeding ₹2,000 must be made by cheque, demand draft, or electronic transfer (UPI/NetBanking). Cash donations exceeding ₹2,000 are NOT eligible for 80GGA deduction.
What is the maximum deduction percentage under 80GGA?
Section 80GGA allows 100% deduction of the donated amount without any upper cap (unlike Section 80G which has 50% / 100% caps with qualifying limits).
Which institutions qualify for Section 80GGA donations?
Qualifying institutions include: Approved Research Associations, Universities/Colleges for Scientific Research, National Urban Poverty Eradication Fund, and Approved Rural Development Funds under Sec 35CCA/35CCB.
What proof is required to claim Section 80GGA?
You need a stamped 80GGA Donation Receipt displaying the institution's 80GGA approval number, PAN, and the donor's PAN and address.
Is Section 80GGA available under the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80GGA is available exclusively under the Old Tax Regime. The New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) does not permit 80GGA deductions.
How does 80GGA differ from 80G?
Section 80G covers general charitable trusts and relief funds (often capped at 50% or 10% of ATI). Section 80GGA specifically covers scientific/rural research donations and provides an uncapped 100% deduction.
