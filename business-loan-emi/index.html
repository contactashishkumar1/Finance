Unsecured Business Loan EMI & Cashflow Planning

  • Working Capital Management: Funds machinery expansion, inventory procurement, and commercial office upgrades without diluting equity.
  • Tax Deductible Interest: Business loan interest payments are 100% tax-deductible as business operating expenses under Section 36(1)(iii).

DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) Eligibility

  • Target DSCR > 1.5x: Lenders evaluate Net Operating Income divided by annual debt service (Principal + Interest). Maintain DSCR above 1.5x for easy approval.
  • Tenure Selection: Choose 3-5 year tenures to keep monthly EMIs within 25% of gross monthly operational business revenue.

Business Financing Insight

Compare unsecured business loans (12-16% interest) against Secured OD/CC against property (8-10% interest) to save significant interest capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is business loan interest tax deductible in India?
Yes, interest paid on a business loan is treated as a business expense and can be claimed as a deduction under Section 37(1) of the Income Tax Act.
What is the average interest rate for business loans in 2026?
Secured business loan rates range from 8.5% to 12% p.a., while unsecured business loans range from 13% to 22% p.a.
What is the processing fee on business loans?
Lenders charge a processing fee of 1% to 3% of the loan amount plus 18% GST.
How is business loan EMI calculated?
EMI = P * r * (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is Loan Principal, r is Monthly Interest Rate, and n is Tenure in months.
Can I get a business loan without collateral?
Yes, unsecured business loans up to ₹50 Lakhs (or CGTMSE guaranteed loans up to ₹5 Crores) are offered by banks based on business turnover and ITR.
What documents are required for a business loan?
2 years ITR with computation, audited P&L and Balance Sheet, 6 months bank statement, GST returns, and business registration proof.
Does principal repayment reduce business income tax?
No, principal repayment is not a business expense. Only the interest component of the EMI is tax-deductible.
What is CGTMSE loan scheme?
CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises) enables banks to lend collateral-free business loans guaranteed by the Govt.
