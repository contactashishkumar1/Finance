Option selling allows traders to capture theta decay (time value erosion) as contracts approach expiration date:

Key Benefits of Option Writing

  • High Win-Rate Probability: Win when market goes up, sideways, or slightly down (70%+ probability of profit).
  • Pledge Margin Capital: Pledge LiquidBEES / Mutual Funds to earn extra 6% debt interest while selling options.

Limitations & Risk Controls (2026)

  • Black-Swan Gap Down Risk: Unhedged short options face severe losses during market panics.
  • SEBI Peak Margin Rules: Margin requirements adjust dynamically during periods of spiking Implied Volatility (IV).

Option Seller Rule of 80%

Close option writing trades when 70-80% of total premium has decayed. Holding options to 0% expiration increases tail-risk for negligible remaining gain!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Return on Margin (ROM) in Option Selling?
Return on Margin (ROM) measures the percentage net profit generated by writing option contracts divided by the SPAN + Exposure margin blocked by the exchange.
How do you annualize Option Writing returns?
Annualized Return % = (Net Premium Profit / Blocked Margin) * (365 / Days to Expiry) * 100.
What margin is required to sell Nifty or BankNifty options?
SEBI peak margin rules require roughly ₹1.2 Lakhs to ₹1.5 Lakhs per naked lot of Nifty or BankNifty options, which can be hedged down to ₹35,000 using Iron Condor or Credit Spread strategies.
What are the STT charges on option selling?
Securities Transaction Tax (STT) is levied at 0.0625% on the option premium when selling option contracts on Indian exchanges.
Why is option selling considered higher win-rate than option buying?
Option sellers profit from theta decay (time decay) and volatility collapse (IV crush), winning when the underlying asset stays flat, moves in their favor, or moves slightly against them.
How does hedging reduce margin requirements for option sellers?
Buying a deep out-of-the-money (OTM) wing option limits maximum tail-risk loss, allowing clearing corporations to grant up to 70% margin benefit on defined-risk spreads.
Can I pledge Mutual Funds or Liquid ETFs for Option Selling margin?
Yes. Indian brokers allow pledging Liquid ETFs (LIQUIDBEES) or Stocks/Mutual Funds (subject to 10-20% haircut) to fulfill 50% cash-equivalent collateral requirements.
What is the risk of tail-risk events in naked option writing?
Unhedged (naked) option selling carries unlimited risk during black-swan market gap-downs. Always trade defined-risk spreads or use stop-loss triggers.
