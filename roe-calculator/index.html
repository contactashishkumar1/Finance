Return on Equity (ROE) Formula & Valuation Impact

  • ROE Formula: (Net Profit After Tax / Shareholder's Equity) * 100.
  • Shareholder Value Metric: Measures how many rupees of net profit a company generates for every ₹100 of equity capital owned by shareholders.

Evaluating High ROE vs Debt Leverage Risks

  • High ROE Target (> 18-20%): Indicates superior pricing power and efficient capital reinvestment.
  • Debt-Artificially Inflated ROE: Beware of companies boosting ROE by taking on massive debt (shrinking equity denominator). Use DuPont Analysis to verify!

ROE Growth Rule

Sustainable long-term earnings growth rate (g) equals ROE multiplied by the Retention Ratio (1 - Dividend Payout %). High ROE businesses compound fastest without needing extra debt!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Return on Equity (ROE)?
Return on Equity (ROE) measures how efficiently a company converts shareholders' invested capital into net profits after tax: ROE = (Net Profit After Tax / Shareholders' Equity) * 100.
What is a good ROE percentage for Indian stocks?
An ROE of 15% to 25% per annum is considered excellent for Indian equity stocks, demonstrating efficient management and strong shareholder returns.
Can a high ROE be misleading?
Yes! A company taking on excessive debt (financial leverage) reduces equity while boosting ROE, creating an illusion of high profitability despite elevated bankruptcy risk.
How to verify if ROE is genuine or debt-driven?
Use DuPont 3-step analysis to break down ROE into Net Profit Margin * Asset Turnover * Financial Leverage.
What is included in Shareholders' Equity?
Shareholders' Equity equals Equity Share Capital + Reserves & Surplus (retained earnings) - Accumulated Losses.
How does share buyback impact ROE?
When a company buys back shares, it reduces net equity balance on the balance sheet, which naturally increases the ROE percentage.
What causes ROE to decline over time?
ROE declines if net profit margins shrink, asset turnover drops, or retained earnings accumulate without generating incremental profits.
What is Sustainable Growth Rate (SGR)?
Sustainable Growth Rate = ROE * (1 - Dividend Payout Ratio). It is the maximum rate a business can grow without issuing new equity or debt.
