EPFO Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS-95) Rules (2026)

The Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) is managed by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) for organized sector employees in India.

Basic vs Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS)

  • Basic EPS: (Net Profit After Tax - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Outstanding Common Shares.
  • Diluted EPS: Factors in potential share dilution from convertible debentures, stock options (ESOPs), and warrants.

Role of EPS in Stock Valuation Metrics

  • P/E Ratio Backbone: Price-to-Earnings = Stock Price / EPS. High EPS growth drives stock price appreciation.
  • Earnings Quality Audit: Ensure EPS growth is driven by core operational revenue rather than one-off asset sales or share buybacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme)?
EPS-95 is a social security pension scheme administered by EPFO. Out of the employer's 12% EPF contribution, 8.33% (up to ₹1,250/mo) goes into the EPS account to provide monthly pension after age 58.
What is the formula to calculate EPS Monthly Pension?
Monthly Pension = (Pensionable Salary * Pensionable Service) / 70.
What is the statutory wage ceiling for EPS pensionable salary?
The standard statutory wage ceiling is ₹15,000 per month. However, employees who opted for Supreme Court's Higher Pension Scheme can calculate pension on actual basic salary.
What is the 2-year bonus rule for EPS pensionable service?
If an employee completes 20 or more years of continuous eligible service under EPS, 2 bonus years are added to their total pensionable service.
Minimum service required to be eligible for EPS pension?
An employee must complete at least 10 years of eligible service to qualify for a monthly pension at age 58.
Can I claim early EPS pension before age 58?
Yes, early pension can be claimed between ages 50 and 58, but the pension amount is reduced by 4% for every year claimed before 58.
What happens to EPS if service is less than 10 years?
If service is less than 10 years, the member is not eligible for monthly pension but can withdraw the full accumulated EPS amount using Form 10C.
Is EPS monthly pension taxable in India?
Yes, monthly EPS pension received after retirement is treated as 'Salary Income' and taxed according to the individual's income tax slab.
