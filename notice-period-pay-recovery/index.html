Notice Pay Recovery & GST Regulations (2026)

When an employee exits before completing the mandatory notice period specified in their employment contract, employers recover notice pay for unserved days during the Full & Final (F&F) settlement.

Calculating Notice Period Buyout & Recovery Deductions

  • Unserved Notice Days Recovery: Per-day basic/gross salary deducted for unserved notice period days upon resignation.
  • 18% GST Penalty Risk: Under recent tax advance rulings, notice recovery deductions made by employers can attract 18% GST if treated as service breach compensation.

New Employer Buyout Taxability

  • Reimbursement Taxability: If your new employer reimburses your notice buyout amount, the reimbursed sum is added to your gross taxable salary income.
  • Net Settlement Credit: Verify Full & Final (FnF) statement calculation against earned leave encashment and pending bonus credits.

Notice Buyout Negotiation Tip

Request your former employer to adjust unserved notice days against accrued EL/PL leaves to minimize cash recovery deductions and GST exposure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Notice Period Pay Recovery?
Notice Period Pay Recovery is the financial deduction made by an employer from an employee's Full & Final (F&F) settlement for leaving the organization without serving the agreed notice period days.
How is Notice Pay Recovery calculated?
Notice Pay Recovery = (Monthly Gross / Basic Salary / 30) * Number of Unserved Notice Days.
Is 18% GST applicable on Notice Pay Recovery in India?
Under GST rulings by CBIC, recovery of notice pay by employer for breach of employment contract is treated as a service and attracts 18% GST unless exempted by specific employer policies.
Can the new employer reimburse my Notice Pay Buyout?
Yes, many hiring companies offer a Notice Period Buyout option, reimbursing the recovery amount deducted by your previous employer.
Is Notice Pay Recovery deductible from income tax?
Currently, Income Tax laws do not allow a direct deduction for notice pay recovered by an employer, meaning TDS is calculated on gross earned salary.
Is notice pay calculated on Gross Salary or Basic Salary?
It depends on company HR policy. Standard IT/Corporate companies calculate notice pay on Gross Monthly Salary (excluding variable performance bonus).
What happens if F&F balance is less than the Notice Recovery amount?
If pending monthly salary and leave encashment in F&F fall short of notice pay recovery, the employee must pay the balance amount via cheque/DD to obtain the relieving letter.
Can notice period be adjusted against earned leaves?
Yes, subject to management approval, earned leaves (EL/PL) can be adjusted against unserved notice days to reduce recovery outgo.
