Understanding Land & Property Area Measurement in India (2026)

Real estate transactions in India utilize a mix of international standard units (Square Feet, Square Meters, Acres) and regional traditional land units (Gaj, Bigha, Guntha, Cent, Ground, Kanal, Kattha).

Carpet Area vs Built-Up Area vs Super Built-Up Area (RERA 2026)

Under RERA (Real Estate Regulation and Development Act), property developers are legally mandated to sell apartments based strictly on RERA Carpet Area:

  • RERA Carpet Area: The net usable floor area of an apartment, excluding internal wall thickness. Includes bedroom, living room, kitchen, and bathroom floor areas.
  • Built-Up Area: Carpet Area + Internal/External Wall Thickness + Balconies. Typically 10-15% larger than carpet area.
  • Super Built-Up Area: Built-up area + Proportional share of common building areas (staircase, elevator shafts, clubhouse, lobby). The extra area added is known as Loading Percentage (typically 25-35%).

Step-by-Step Worked Conversion Examples

Worked Example 1: Converting a 30 ft × 50 ft Residential Plot

• Step 1: Plot Area in Sq. Ft. = 30 ft × 50 ft = 1,500 Sq. Ft.
• Step 2: Convert to Gaj = 1,500 ÷ 9 = 166.67 Gaj.
• Step 3: Convert to Cents = 1,500 ÷ 435.6 = 3.44 Cents.
• Step 4: If plot costs ₹60 Lakhs, Rate per Sq. Ft. = ₹60,00,000 ÷ 1,500 = ₹4,000/sq.ft.

Worked Example 2: Converting Agricultural Land (2.5 Acres) into Bigha

• Step 1: 2.5 Acres in Sq. Ft. = 2.5 × 43,560 = 108,900 Sq. Ft.
• Step 2: Convert to UP Pucca Bigha = 108,900 ÷ 27,225 = 4.0 Bigha.
• Step 3: Convert to Maharashtra Gunthas = 108,900 ÷ 1,089 = 100 Gunthas.

Limitations & Important Real Estate Rules (2026)

  • Regional Variations in Bigha: Bigha sizes differ significantly by state! 1 Bigha in UP is 27,225 Sq. Ft., whereas 1 Bigha in West Bengal is 14,400 Sq. Ft. Always verify local revenue records.
  • Super Built-Up vs Carpet Area: RERA rules require property transactions to be billed on Net Carpet Area, not Super Built-Up Area.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Square Feet are in 1 Gaj (Square Yard)?
1 Gaj (or Square Yard) is equal to exactly 9 Square Feet (Sq. Ft.). For example, a 100 Gaj plot equals 900 Sq. Ft.
How many Square Feet are in 1 Acre?
1 Acre is equal to 43,560 Square Feet (or 4,840 Gaj / Square Yards).
What is 1 Guntha in Square Feet?
In South & West India (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Gujarat), 1 Guntha is equal to 1,089 Square Feet. There are 40 Gunthas in 1 Acre.
How many Square Feet make 1 Cent of land?
In Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka, 1 Cent is equal to 435.6 Square Feet. 100 Cents equal 1 Acre.
What is 1 Ground in Tamil Nadu land measurement?
In Tamil Nadu real estate, 1 Ground is equal to 2,400 Square Feet (or 266.67 Gaj).
How many Square Feet in 1 Bigha in UP vs Bengal?
In Uttar Pradesh (Pucca Bigha), 1 Bigha equals 27,225 Sq. Ft. (3,000 Gaj). In West Bengal and Bihar, 1 Bigha equals 14,400 Sq. Ft. (1,600 Gaj).
How do you calculate land price per Square Foot from total price?
Land Price per Sq. Ft. = Total Property Purchase Price / Total Area in Sq. Ft. This converter automatically computes price per Sq. Ft., Gaj, and Acre!
What is the difference between RERA Carpet Area and Super Built-Up Area?
RERA Carpet Area is the actual usable net floor area of an apartment excluding internal walls. Built-up area includes wall thickness and balcony. Super Built-Up area includes common areas like staircases, lobbies, and elevators (usually 25-35% loading).
What is 1 Kanal and 1 Marla in Punjab and Haryana?
1 Kanal equals 5,445 Square Feet (or 20 Marlas). 1 Marla equals 272.25 Square Feet. 8 Kanals equal 1 Acre.
