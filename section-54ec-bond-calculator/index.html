Section 54EC Bond Rules & Limits (2026)

Section 54EC of the Income Tax Act grants tax exemption on Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) arising from the sale of land or building when invested in specified infrastructure bonds:

Parameter Section 54EC Bond Terms
Eligible Asset Sold Land or Building (Residential / Commercial Plot / House)
Approved Bond Issuers NHAI, REC, PFC, and IRFC
Statutory Investment Ceiling ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs) per Financial Year
Lock-in Period & Coupon Rate 5-Year Lock-in | Fixed 5.25% p.a. Interest

Key Benefits of 54EC Bonds

  • 100% Tax Relief on LTCG: Saves 12.5% LTCG tax on real estate profit up to ₹50 Lakhs.
  • AAA Sovereign Security: Fully backed by Govt of India undertakings (NHAI, REC, PFC).

Limitations & Opportunity Cost (2026)

  • Taxable Interest Income: The 5.25% interest is fully taxable under your slab rate (~3.61% post-tax yield for 30% slab).
  • Opportunity Cost vs Equity: If equity markets yield 12% CAGR, paying 12.5% tax upfront and investing in mutual funds often generates higher 5-year wealth!

54EC Decision Rule

If you are risk-averse and prioritize guaranteed capital protection, choose 54EC bonds. If you seek maximum 5-year net wealth, pay the 12.5% LTCG tax and invest in index funds!

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Section 54EC Capital Gains Bonds?
Section 54EC bonds are government-backed infrastructure bonds issued by NHAI, REC, PFC, and IRFC that allow property sellers to save 12.5% LTCG tax by investing long-term capital gains.
What is the maximum investment limit under Section 54EC?
The statutory investment ceiling under Section 54EC is ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs) per financial year across all eligible issuers.
What is the lock-in tenure for 54EC bonds?
54EC bonds have a mandatory 5-year lock-in period. They cannot be sold, transferred, or pledged as loan collateral during this 5-year window.
What is the interest rate on 54EC bonds?
The coupon interest rate on 54EC bonds is fixed at 5.25% per annum, payable annually on April 1st.
Is 54EC bond interest income taxable?
Yes. While the capital invested saves capital gains tax, the 5.25% annual interest income is fully taxable at your applicable income tax slab rate.
What is the timeline to invest in Section 54EC bonds after property sale?
The taxpayer must invest in 54EC bonds within 6 months from the date of transfer of the real estate property.
Which assets qualify for Section 54EC tax exemption?
Only long-term capital gains arising from the sale of Land or Building (residential or commercial) qualify for Section 54EC exemption. Equity/gold gains do not qualify.
Should I pay 12.5% LTCG tax and invest in Mutual Funds instead of 54EC bonds?
If equity mutual funds generate 12%+ CAGR over 5 years, paying 12.5% LTCG tax upfront and investing the remaining 87.5% capital often yields higher 5-year net wealth than 5.25% fixed 54EC bonds.
