2-Stage DCF Valuation Principles (2026)

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) calculates the present value of all future cash flows expected to be generated by a business.

DCF Stage Calculation Method Role in Intrinsic Price
Stage 1: Explicit 5-Yr Forecast FCFF_t / (1 + WACC)^t Captures short-term high growth phase
Stage 2: Terminal Value (TV) [FCFF_5 * (1 + g)] / (WACC - g) Captures perpetual value beyond year 5 (discounted to PV)

2-Stage Discounted Free Cash Flow (FCFF) Modeling

  • Stage 1 (Explicit Forecast): Discount projected Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) for 5-10 years at WACC discount rate.
  • Stage 2 (Terminal Value): Perpetual valuation beyond year 5: TV = [FCFF_5 * (1 + g)] / (WACC - g), discounted back to present value.
  • Equity Value Conversion: Equity Value = Enterprise Value + Cash - Total Debt. Intrinsic Share Price = Equity Value / Total Shares.

Key Assumptions & Sensitivity Risks

  • WACC Sensitivity: A 1% change in WACC discount rate can alter intrinsic share price by 15-20%.
  • Terminal Growth Cap: Terminal growth rate (g) should never exceed long-term GDP growth rate (capped at 4-5% in India).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation?
DCF Valuation estimates the intrinsic true value of an enterprise by discounting its expected future Free Cash Flows (FCFF) back to their present value using WACC.
What is Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)?
FCFF represents cash generated by core operations available to all capital providers after deducting operating expenses, taxes, working capital investments, and capital expenditure (capex).
What is Terminal Value in DCF?
Terminal Value (TV) estimates the total value of all cash flows beyond the explicit 5-10 year forecast period into perpetuity using the Gordon Growth Model.
What terminal growth rate should be used for Indian companies?
Terminal growth rate should not exceed long-term GDP growth. In India, a perpetual terminal growth rate of 4.0% to 5.0% is standard.
How is Enterprise Value converted to Equity Value per Share?
Equity Value = Enterprise Value + Cash & Cash Equivalents - Total Debt. Intrinsic Value per Share = Equity Value / Outstanding Shares.
What is Margin of Safety in DCF valuation?
Margin of Safety is the percentage discount between the intrinsic DCF value and current market price. Benjamin Graham recommended a 20%-30% margin of safety.
Why is WACC used as the discount rate?
WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital) reflects the minimum rate of return required by both equity investors and debt lenders to finance the firm's assets.
What are the key limitations of DCF model?
DCF is highly sensitive to small changes in WACC and terminal growth rate assumptions ('garbage in, garbage out'). It should always be paired with relative P/E valuation.
