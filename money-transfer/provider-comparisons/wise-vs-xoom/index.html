Executive Summary

Wise vs Xoom: Quick Overview

Both Wise and Xoom are digital-first remittance platforms, but they operate on fundamentally different pricing philosophies and target different customer needs.

The Transparent Leader

Wise Overview

Wise is renowned for transparency. They give you the actual mid-market exchange rate (the one you see on Google) and charge a low, upfront percentage fee. They specialize in bank-to-bank transfers and multi-currency accounts.

The PayPal Subsidiary

Xoom Overview

Xoom (a PayPal service) focuses on speed, convenience, and reach in developing countries, including cash pickup options. However, they charge both an upfront fee and heavily markup the exchange rate, making them significantly more expensive.

Quick Verdict

If you are sending money to a bank account, Wise may be the better option due to highly competitive exchange rates and lower fees. Choose Xoom only if you absolutely need cash pickup, want to pay with your PayPal balance, or are paying international utility bills.

Fees & Hidden Markups

Wise: Total Transparency

Wise charges a single, visible fee based on the transfer amount and route. Because they use the real mid-market exchange rate, the fee you see on the screen is the absolute total cost of the transfer.

Xoom: The Double Charge

Xoom utilizes a less favorable pricing model. First, they charge an upfront fixed transaction fee (which varies depending on how you fund it—bank account vs. debit card). Second, and more importantly, Xoom marks up the exchange rate. Xoom explicitly states in their terms: "In addition to the transaction fee, Xoom also makes money when it changes your US dollars into a different currency." This markup can easily exceed 2% of your transfer value.

Who Should Choose Wise?

  • • Anyone transferring directly to a bank account.
  • • Users who want the highly competitive cost and highly competitive exchange rate.
  • • Senders transferring large amounts (where Xoom's percentage markup is costly).

Who Should Choose Xoom?

  • • Users who want to fund a transfer with their PayPal balance.
  • • Senders whose recipients require cash pickup.
  • • Users looking to pay utility bills directly in another country.
