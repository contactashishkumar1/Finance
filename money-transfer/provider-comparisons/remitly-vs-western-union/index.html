Executive Summary

Remitly vs Western Union: Quick Overview

Remitly and Western Union both enable people to send money internationally, but they represent two fundamentally different eras and approaches to money transfer. Remitly is a digitally native remittance platform built for smartphones and web access, designed to offer competitive pricing and multiple delivery options across major corridors. Western Union is one of the world's oldest and most recognisable financial services brands, built on a global retail agent network that enables cash-based transactions anywhere in the world.

Digital Remittance Platform

Remitly

App and web-first platform focused on personal remittances across major corridors. Supports bank deposit, mobile wallet delivery, cash pickup, and in select markets, home delivery.

Global Retail Agent Network

Western Union

Globally established money transfer company with one of the largest agent networks in the world. Supports cash-in at retail locations and cash pickup by recipients in over 200 countries and territories.

The fundamental choice between them often comes down to recipient access: does your recipient need cash at a local agent, or can they receive a bank deposit or mobile wallet payment? The answer to that question shapes which provider is more suitable for a given transfer.

Platform Profiles

Detailed Provider Overviews

Remitly

Remitly was founded in Seattle in 2011 and has grown into one of the leading digital remittance platforms serving immigrant communities sending money back to their home countries. It operates as an app and web-first service, requiring users to register a digital account. Remitly supports multiple delivery methods in covered corridors: bank deposit, cash pickup through partner agents, mobile wallet delivery, and in select markets, home delivery.

Remitly's pricing typically involves an exchange-rate spread and sometimes an upfront fee, with two transfer speed options — Economy and Express — that affect both cost and delivery timing. Remitly has expanded its corridor coverage significantly and now serves many of the world's highest-volume remittance routes, including US to Mexico, Philippines, India, and others.

Remitly is publicly listed on NASDAQ and regulated as a money services business in the US and holds licences in other markets. Its growth has been driven by the expansion of mobile internet access in sending countries and growing immigrant population demographics.

Western Union

Western Union was founded in 1851 and has been a global money transfer institution for over a century. It operates one of the world's most extensive agent networks, with hundreds of thousands of locations including banks, post offices, convenience stores, pharmacies, and dedicated money transfer outlets across more than 200 countries and territories.

Western Union has evolved with technology, offering digital transfer channels through its website and mobile app. However, its defining competitive advantage remains the retail agent network, which provides unmatched geographic reach for cash-based transfers. Recipients who cannot or do not want to use banking infrastructure can collect cash at a nearby Western Union agent using a unique transaction code provided to the sender.

Western Union is publicly traded on the NYSE and regulated across its global markets. Its pricing model includes both upfront fees and an exchange-rate spread, with costs varying by delivery method and channel. In-store and digital pricing may differ materially on the same corridor.

Cost Structures

Pricing Philosophy

Remitly: Tiered Speed-Based Pricing

Remitly structures its pricing around two transfer speed tiers. The Economy option typically offers a more favourable exchange rate with a lower or waived fee, but delivers funds more slowly. The Express option delivers faster and may offer cash pickup at partner agents, but the pricing — whether through the exchange-rate spread or an explicit fee — generally reflects this premium.

The total cost of a Remitly transfer is shown before confirmation, allowing senders to understand the net destination amount before committing. Pricing varies by corridor, payment method, and transfer amount. Senders who are not time-sensitive may find the Economy tier offers better overall value.

Western Union: Channel-Dependent Pricing

Western Union's pricing model is notably channel-dependent. Transfers initiated at retail agent locations in person typically cost more than equivalent transfers sent through the digital app or website. Both an upfront transfer fee and an exchange-rate spread contribute to the total cost, though their relative weighting varies by corridor and channel.

This means that two people sending the same amount to the same country via Western Union may pay materially different effective costs depending on whether they use an agent location, the website, or the app. Senders who use the digital channel generally receive more competitive pricing.

Comparing the net payout in the destination currency — rather than focusing on stated fees alone — is the most reliable way to evaluate the true cost of a Western Union transfer against alternatives including Remitly.

Rate Mechanisms

Exchange-Rate Philosophy

Neither Remitly nor Western Union passes the mid-market exchange rate to senders. Both embed a margin within the exchange rate they offer, which is a standard practice for consumer remittance services that have broader operational cost structures than specialist rate-transparency platforms like Wise.

Remitly's approach: The exchange rate applied to a Remitly transfer includes a margin above the interbank rate. The size of this margin varies by corridor and transfer type (Economy vs Express). Remitly shows the destination amount clearly on its app before the transfer is confirmed, making it relatively straightforward for senders to assess value.

Western Union's approach: Western Union similarly embeds a margin in its exchange rate. Because Western Union prices differently across its retail and digital channels, the effective exchange rate may differ between sending methods. Historically, in-store transfers have often offered less favourable exchange rates than online equivalents.

For both providers, calculating the net amount the recipient will receive — and comparing that figure against other providers — is the most reliable measure of true cost.

Delivery Channels

Funding & Delivery Channels

Remitly

  • • Sender funding: Bank transfer, debit card, credit card (card fees may apply)
  • • Bank deposit: Direct to recipient bank account
  • • Cash pickup: Via partner agent network
  • • Mobile wallet: Select corridors in Africa, Asia, Latin America
  • • Home delivery: Select corridors

Western Union

  • • Sender funding: Cash at agent, bank transfer, debit/credit card, online banking
  • • Cash pickup: Hundreds of thousands of agent locations globally
  • • Bank deposit: Supported in many countries
  • • Mobile wallet: Available in select markets
  • • Home delivery: Select corridors

Western Union's defining advantage is its cash-at-agent capability for senders. This allows individuals without a bank account or digital payment method to walk into a retail location with cash and send money internationally. Remitly does not offer this capability — all Remitly transfers must be funded digitally.

User Journey

Transfer Experience

Remitly's digital-native experience is purpose-built for smartphones. The app is intuitive and optimised for the remittance use case: choose a recipient, select delivery method, enter amount, confirm cost, and send. The app provides real-time tracking and push notifications at each stage. The Economy/Express toggle allows users to immediately see how the pricing changes with speed selection.

Western Union's experience spans both physical and digital channels. The mobile app and website are functional and have improved substantially over recent years. Users initiating digital transfers through Western Union's app or website receive a similar pre-confirmation cost display. In-person agent transactions involve completing a transfer form, presenting identification, and paying with cash or card at the counter.

For digital senders, Remitly's streamlined, remittance-focused interface generally offers a more modern and intuitive experience. Western Union's digital channel is effective but reflects its dual heritage as both a retail and digital service. For in-person senders, Western Union has no meaningful digital-first competitor at scale.

Recipient Access

Recipient Flexibility

Recipient flexibility is perhaps the most important differentiator between these two providers. It determines which provider is actually usable for a given transfer given the recipient's circumstances.

Urban recipients with bank accounts: Both platforms serve urban, banked recipients well through bank deposit delivery. Remitly's digital-native pricing may offer more competitive rates in some corridors for this scenario.

Rural recipients without bank accounts: Western Union's agent network provides significantly better coverage in rural and underserved areas. A recipient in a small town or village without reliable banking access is far more likely to find a Western Union agent within practical distance than a Remitly cash pickup partner.

Recipients with mobile money accounts: Both providers support mobile wallet delivery in select markets. Remitly has invested in mobile wallet partnerships across Africa, South Asia, and Latin America. Western Union also offers mobile wallet delivery in a growing number of markets. Coverage varies by corridor.

Recipients who prefer cash: Western Union's cash pickup remains the most reliable and widely available option for recipients who prefer to receive physical cash or who live in markets where digital financial infrastructure is limited.

Safety & Compliance

Security, Compliance & Safeguarding

Both providers are publicly listed companies operating under significant regulatory scrutiny in their respective markets. Remitly (NASDAQ: RELY) is regulated as a money services business in the US and holds licences in other markets. Western Union (NYSE: WU) is one of the most extensively regulated money transfer companies in the world, operating under oversight from regulators across dozens of countries.

Both providers are required to maintain compliance programmes including Anti-Money Laundering (AML) procedures, Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification, and suspicious activity reporting. Digital account holders on both platforms complete identity verification before sending. In-person Western Union senders may be required to present physical identification above certain transfer amounts.

Users should be aware that both platforms have been used as vehicles for scams in the past — particularly payment fraud where fraudsters impersonate legitimate organisations and instruct victims to send money. Neither provider endorses unsolicited payment requests, and users should exercise caution with any transfer they did not themselves initiate.

Support Models

Customer Support

Remitly provides customer support through in-app messaging, email, and telephone in supported markets. Given that it is a digital-only sender channel, Remitly's support infrastructure is geared around resolving digital transfer queries. Support quality has generally improved as the platform has scaled.

Western Union offers multiple support channels reflecting its hybrid retail-digital model: telephone customer service lines, online chat, in-app support, and in-person assistance at retail agent locations. The retail agent network effectively provides a distributed human support layer unavailable to purely digital platforms. Recipients can also receive assistance at agent locations when collecting cash transfers.

Scenario Analysis

Practical Use Cases

Monthly support to family with a bank account: Remitly's Economy tier typically offers competitive all-in pricing on high-volume corridors when timing flexibility allows. Bank deposit delivery is reliable and avoids the recipient having to travel to collect cash.
Sending to a rural village with no bank branch: Western Union is generally the stronger option when the recipient is in a rural location where no bank branch or digital wallet exists. A Western Union agent at a local post office or shop may be the most accessible collection point.
Emergency transfer where cash is needed immediately: Western Union's cash pickup network means a recipient can often collect funds within minutes of the sender completing the transfer. This makes it suitable for genuine emergencies where immediate cash access is critical.
Sending money from a country with limited digital payment access: Western Union's retail agent network allows senders without bank accounts or digital payment infrastructure to initiate a transfer with cash at a local agent location.
Paying university fees for a student in another country: Both platforms support bank deposit for this use case. Remitly's Economy tier may offer better value for a non-urgent payment where the net payout comparison favours it. Western Union's digital channel is also suitable.
Supporting elderly parents who are not comfortable with digital banking: Cash pickup via Western Union is accessible for recipients who do not use smartphones or digital financial tools. The recipient simply provides their identification at the agent and collects cash using the transfer reference number.
Sending to a recipient with a mobile money wallet in Kenya or Ghana: Remitly has established mobile wallet delivery partnerships in several African markets. Western Union also supports mobile wallet delivery in select African corridors. Both may be suitable; users should check corridor-specific availability on each platform.

Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between Remitly and Western Union?
Remitly is a digital-first platform designed primarily for smartphone or web-based remittances with multiple delivery options. Western Union is a long-established global money transfer company with an extensive retail agent network enabling cash pickup at hundreds of thousands of locations worldwide.
Can my recipient pick up cash from a Remitly transfer?
Yes. Remitly supports cash pickup in many corridors through local agent partnerships. However, Western Union's cash pickup network is significantly larger in terms of global agent locations, particularly in rural areas.
Does Western Union offer a digital transfer option?
Yes. Western Union has invested in its digital platform and mobile app. Senders can initiate transfers online without visiting a retail location. Recipients can still collect cash at an agent even when the sender used the digital channel.
Which provider is better for sending money to rural areas?
Western Union's extensive global agent network generally provides better coverage in rural and remote areas where banking infrastructure may be limited.
Does Remitly support mobile wallet delivery?
Yes. Remitly supports mobile wallet delivery in a growing number of markets, particularly in Africa, South and Southeast Asia, and Latin America, allowing recipients without bank accounts to receive money via mobile money services.
Which provider is more suitable for regular family remittances?
Remitly is generally designed with regular family remittances in mind. Western Union is also used for regular remittances, particularly where cash pickup is the preferred delivery method for the recipient.
Can I use Western Union without a smartphone?
Yes. Western Union can be accessed at retail agent locations without a smartphone. Remitly is primarily a digital product requiring app or web access to initiate transfers.
Which provider is better for emergency transfers?
For genuine emergencies where a recipient needs cash immediately, Western Union's agent network may allow near-immediate cash collection in many countries. Remitly's Express option also delivers in supported corridors. The appropriate choice depends on what delivery method the recipient can access.
How does pricing transparency compare between the two?
Remitly generally shows the total cost including exchange-rate spread before confirmation. Western Union's pricing varies by delivery method and channel; in-store costs may differ materially from online or app-initiated transfers.
Which provider has more agent locations globally?
Western Union has one of the largest agent networks in the world with locations across over 200 countries and territories. Remitly's cash pickup partner network is smaller but covers major remittance corridors.
Is Remitly regulated?
Yes. Remitly is regulated as a money services business in the US and holds licences in markets including the UK, EU, Canada, and Australia. Both Remitly and Western Union operate under regulatory oversight in their respective markets.
Can recipients receive funds without a bank account via Remitly?
Yes. Remitly supports cash pickup and mobile wallet delivery in many corridors, allowing recipients without formal bank accounts to receive funds through alternative channels.
Which provider is more suitable for an unbanked sender?
Western Union is more accessible for unbanked senders because transfers can be initiated with cash at a retail agent location. Remitly requires digital account registration and typically requires a bank account or debit/credit card to fund transfers.
Does Western Union support bank deposit delivery?
Yes. Western Union supports bank deposit delivery in many countries, allowing recipients to receive funds directly into their bank accounts. Remitly also supports bank deposit across a wide range of corridors.
Which platform is better for sending to Latin America?
Both providers have strong coverage in Latin America. Western Union has an extensive agent network across the region. Remitly supports bank deposit and mobile wallet delivery in many Latin American markets. Users should compare net payouts for their specific corridor.
What happens if a Western Union transfer is not collected?
Uncollected Western Union cash transfers are held for a period and can then be cancelled and refunded to the sender. The exact timeframe and process may vary by corridor. Senders should contact Western Union directly if a transfer remains uncollected.
Can I send money using a credit card on Remitly?
Remitly accepts credit card funding in supported markets. Additional fees may apply when funding by credit card. Bank transfer or debit card funding typically carries lower fees on most remittance platforms.
Does Western Union have a mobile app?
Yes. Western Union offers a mobile app for initiating digital transfers, tracking payments, and managing recipient details. The app complements rather than replaces the traditional retail agent network.
Is Western Union suitable for business payments?
Western Union's core consumer service is primarily designed for personal remittances. Business payment solutions were historically offered under WU Business Solutions, which has since been rebranded as Convera — a separate entity.
How do both platforms verify sender identity?
Both platforms require identity verification. Digital accounts on both Remitly and Western Union's app require KYC verification with photo ID. In-person Western Union senders may be required to present physical identification at the agent counter above certain transfer amounts.
Which provider offers better value for small transfers?
For small transfers, fixed fees become proportionally more significant. Remitly's Economy tier may offer competitive pricing on small amounts in major corridors. Western Union's in-store fees may be less competitive on small transfers. Users should compare net payouts on both platforms for their specific corridor and amount.
Does Remitly offer home delivery?
Remitly supports home delivery in select corridors. Western Union also offers home delivery in certain markets. Availability depends on the specific destination country and corridor.
Which provider should I choose if I am sending money for the first time?
The answer depends on how your recipient would like to receive the money. If they have a bank account and you are comfortable using an app, Remitly is worth comparing. If your recipient needs cash or prefers collecting from a local agent, Western Union is the more established option. Comparing net payouts on both platforms for your specific transfer is always recommended.
Editorial Conclusion

Final Thoughts

Remitly and Western Union serve the same fundamental purpose — helping people support their families and communities across borders — but through very different operational models built for different user contexts.

Remitly is a compelling choice for digitally enabled senders who want a modern app experience, competitive pricing on major corridors, and the flexibility to deliver funds via bank deposit, mobile wallet, or cash pickup. It is particularly well-suited to senders whose recipients hold bank accounts or mobile money wallets in covered markets.

Western Union's defining strength is its geographic reach. No other provider matches its ability to put cash in the hands of recipients in rural and remote communities across more than 200 countries. For senders without digital payment access, or for recipients in areas with limited banking infrastructure, Western Union often remains the most practical — and sometimes the only — viable option.

The right choice depends fundamentally on recipient circumstances. We recommend considering how your recipient is best able to access the funds, and then comparing the net payout from each platform for your specific corridor and amount.
