Instarem vs Remitly: Money Transfer Guide

An objective guide comparing Instarem's reward-based digital transfers against Remitly's expansive cash pickup and immigrant remittance network.

Executive Summary

Quick Answer

Instarem (Digital Fintech)

Instarem is a highly competitive digital transfer service that offers exchange rates very close to the mid-market rate. They are famous for their InstaPoints loyalty program, which allows frequent users to discount their transfer fees. They only support bank-to-bank electronic transfers.

Remitly (Remittance Specialist)

Remitly focuses explicitly on immigrant communities sending money back home. They offer a massive physical network for cash pickups, mobile wallet deposits, and home delivery in select nations. They utilize a tiered pricing model (Economy vs Express) and apply a variable exchange rate markup.

Key Takeaway

If your recipient needs to pick up physical cash or receive money into a mobile wallet, Remitly is the clear choice. If you are sending money strictly between bank accounts (especially to Asian countries), Instarem is usually cheaper and their loyalty program rewards frequent senders.

Provider Introductions

Instarem, founded in Singapore, was built to eliminate opaque foreign exchange margins in the Asia-Pacific region and beyond. By focusing entirely on digital, bank-to-bank transfers, they operate with very low overhead. Their standout feature is "InstaPoints," a rewards system that gamifies international transfers by giving you points that convert into direct fee discounts.

Remitly, based in Seattle, is a dominant force in the global remittance industry. They understand that in many developing nations, a bank account isn't always accessible. Therefore, Remitly has partnered with thousands of global banks, retail stores, and pawnshops to allow recipients to collect hard cash. They also offer flexible delivery speeds depending on how much you are willing to pay.

Fees & Exchange Rates

When comparing costs, you have to look at the total combination of upfront fees and hidden exchange rate markups.

Instarem's Transparent Pricing

Instarem prides itself on offering rates incredibly close to the Reuters mid-market rate. Instead of hiding profit in the exchange rate, they charge a transparent percentage fee (usually between 0.25% and 1%, depending on the corridor). However, the real cost-saver is InstaPoints. You earn points for joining, referring, and transferring. Redeeming these points can bring the effective cost of a transfer down to almost zero.

Remitly's Tiered Pricing

Remitly offers flexibility. Their "Economy" tier (funded via your bank account) often has very low or zero upfront fees, but it takes 3-5 days to deliver. Their "Express" tier (funded via debit card) arrives in minutes but charges a higher upfront fee. Regardless of the tier, Remitly applies a markup to the exchange rate (usually between 1% and 2.5%). Remitly does offer incredibly aggressive promotional rates for your very first transfer.

Important Note

For recurring monthly transfers, Instarem is often highly cost-effective because you will continually accumulate and spend InstaPoints. Remitly is only cheaper on the very first promotional transfer.

Practical Scenarios

Scenario A: Sending $500 to a Bank Account in India

You need to send money directly to a family member's checking account in Mumbai.

  • Instarem: Near mid-market rate + small transparent fee. You earn points for the next transfer.
  • Remitly: Good promotional rate for the first transfer, but subsequent transfers will incur a markup margin.
  • Winner: Instarem (Better long-term value).

Scenario B: Sending $300 to the Philippines for Cash Pickup

Your relative needs physical cash urgently to pay a local bill and doesn't have a modern bank account.

  • Instarem: Cannot facilitate this transfer at all (bank-to-bank only).
  • Remitly: Allows the recipient to pick up cash instantly at thousands of locations including BDO, SM Malls, and Cebuana Lhuillier.
  • Winner: Remitly (The only choice for cash pickups).

Security & User Experience

Both applications offer highly rated mobile apps (4.5+ stars on iOS and Android). They utilize bank-level encryption and are strictly regulated. Remitly operates as a publicly traded company in the US, giving it intense regulatory oversight. Instarem is regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) and holds licenses in the US, UK, and Australia.

Ease of Use: Remitly's interface is specifically designed for immigrants and non-technical users, making it incredibly simple to use, with robust multi-lingual support. Instarem's app is sleek and functional, though keeping track of the InstaPoints system adds a very minor layer of complexity.

Pros and Cons

Instarem

Pros:
  • Highly competitive, near-mid-market exchange rates
  • InstaPoints loyalty program significantly lowers costs
  • Excellent speeds to Asian corridors
Cons:
  • Bank account transfers only
  • No cash pickup or mobile wallet delivery
  • Limited destination coverage compared to Remitly

Remitly

Pros:
  • Massive global cash pickup network
  • Supports delivery to mobile wallets (like GCash)
  • Flexible Economy vs Express pricing
  • Excellent promotional rates for first-time users
Cons:
  • Applies an opaque markup to the exchange rate
  • Economy transfers can be very slow (3-5 days)

Final Verdict: Which should you choose?

Choose Instarem if: You are transferring money directly from your bank account to the recipient's bank account, especially if you plan to send money regularly. The InstaPoints reward system will make your subsequent transfers cheaper, and you won't lose money to hidden exchange rate markups.

Choose Remitly if: Your recipient lives in a developing country and relies on physical cash or mobile wallets (like M-Pesa or GCash). Instarem simply cannot perform these services. Remitly is also worth using for a one-off transfer to take advantage of their aggressive new-user promotional rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Instarem cheaper than Remitly?
Generally, yes. Instarem uses a highly transparent pricing model with exchange rates very close to the true mid-market rate, and you can further reduce fees using InstaPoints. Remitly adds a markup to their exchange rates.
Which provider is faster?
Remitly's 'Express' tier (funded by debit card) delivers money within minutes. Instarem is also fast, with most bank-to-bank transfers to Asia arriving instantly or within one business day.
Can I pick up cash with Instarem?
No. Instarem only facilitates bank-to-bank transfers. If your recipient needs physical cash, Remitly may be the better option with thousands of retail pickup locations.
How do InstaPoints work?
Instarem rewards users with InstaPoints for signing up, referring friends, and completing transfers. These points can be redeemed on future transfers to discount the fee.
Are both providers safe?
Yes. Remitly is publicly traded on the NASDAQ and regulated heavily globally. Instarem is regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) and other financial watchdogs.
