US to Canada Money Transfer Guide

Best ways to send money from US to Canada. Compare USD to CAD rates, cross-border banking (RBC, TD), ACH to EFT, Wise, and Norbert's Gambit for large sums.

Introduction to the USD-CAD Corridor

The United States and Canada share the largest bilateral trade relationship in the world, with massive cross-border capital flows between businesses, snowbirds, dual citizens, and remote workers.

Converting US Dollars (USD) to Canadian Dollars (CAD) involves unique banking integrations between the US Federal Reserve (ACH/Fedwire) and Payments Canada (EFT/Interac). Major cross-border banks (TD, RBC, BMO) offer unified accounts, but their 2.0% - 2.5% FX markup makes fintech alternatives essential for smart money management.

Why This Corridor is Unique

The USD to CAD remittance corridor stands out for several structural reasons:

  • Integrated Cross-Border Banking Ecosystem: Major Canadian banks (RBC Bank US, TD Bank US, CIBC Bank USA, BMO Harris) offer seamless single-sign-on banking with linked USD and CAD accounts.
  • Norbert's Gambit for Large Conversions: Savvy Canadian investors converting sums over $20,000 can use Norbert's Gambit (buying dual-listed ETFs like DLR.TO on brokerage platforms) to convert currency at virtually 0% FX spread.
  • FinCEN & FINTRAC Regulatory Compliance: Transfers exceeding $10,000 USD/CAD are automatically reported to US FinCEN and Canadian FINTRAC to combat money laundering, requiring complete identity verification.

Popular Transfer Methods

Digital Remittance Specialists

Companies like Wise and Instarem dominate this route by utilizing local clearing bank networks, offering benchmark mid-market rates and low upfront fees.

Traditional SWIFT Bank Wires

Direct international wire transfers between retail commercial banks. High security for large sums, but generally the most expensive due to wire fees and FX markups.

Instant Account / UPI Rail Transfers

Pushing funds directly to recipient bank accounts or virtual payment addresses (e.g. UPI / GCash) for instant settlement in seconds.

Cash Pickup Agent Networks

Networks like Western Union allow physical cash collection at agent counters across the destination country for unbanked recipients.

Typical Transfer Costs

Transferring USD to CAD incurs two potential costs: upfront service fees and hidden exchange rate spreads.

Digital Providers: Digital providers (Wise, OFX) charge total costs between 0.3% and 0.5%. On a $2,000 USD transfer, Wise costs ~$8.40 with exact mid-market exchange rates.

Traditional Retail Banks: Cross-border retail bank accounts charge 2.0% to 2.5% in currency conversion margins. On a $10,000 transfer, a bank spread costs $200 to $250 in lost value.

The USD/CAD pair is one of the most traded currency pairs globally. Interbank spreads are ultra-tight. Choose providers that transparently disclose their margin above the wholesale mid-market rate.

Transfer Speed Expectations

Transfer settlement speed depends primarily on how you fund the transaction in USD:

  • ACH Direct Debit: Takes 1 to 2 business days to clear in the US before instant EFT disbursement in Canada.
  • Cross-Border Linked Accounts: Instant transfer between linked US and Canadian accounts at RBC or TD (subject to bank FX rate).
  • Fedwire Domestic Wire: Same-day clearing in the US with direct Canadian bank settlement within 24 hours.

Payment Methods (USD)

Primary options for funding transfers in USD:

  • ACH Direct Debit: Cheapest funding method from US checking accounts; free or low-cost.
  • Domestic Fedwire: Fast wire execution for large sums over $10,000 USD.
  • US Debit Card: Instant payment processing for quick personal transfers.

Receiving Methods (CAD)

How beneficiaries receive CAD funds:

  • Direct EFT Deposit: Direct electronic funds transfer into any Canadian bank account (TD, RBC, Scotiabank, BMO).
  • Interac Direct Autodeposit: Email-linked direct deposit into Canadian chequing accounts.
  • Canadian USD Account: Hold USD directly in Canada without converting to CAD.

Regulatory Environment

Both sending and receiving jurisdictions enforce strict financial regulations to prevent money laundering and ensure taxation compliance.

In Origin Jurisdiction: Regulated by US FinCEN. Wires over $10,000 USD trigger mandatory CTR / SAR reporting.

In Destination Jurisdiction: In Canada, financial institutions report international transfers of $10,000 CAD or more to FINTRAC within 15 days.

Tips to Reduce Costs

  • Use Norbert's Gambit for $20k+: For major conversions over $20,000, Norbert's Gambit on a Canadian brokerage saves thousands in FX spread.
  • Open a Canadian USD Account: If you receive USD regularly, hold funds in a Canadian USD account until rates improve.
  • Link via ACH Rather Than Wire: ACH funding is free, whereas outbound US wires cost $30 to $45.

Common Mistakes

  • Using Branch Wire Transfers: Sending wires directly from retail US bank branches incurs the highest wire fees and widest FX margins.
  • Failing FBAR Reporting: US citizens holding over $10,000 in Canadian accounts must file annual FBAR with the IRS.

Step-by-Step: Transferring USD to CAD Without Bank Wire Spreads

Follow this optimal cross-border transfer guide to convert USD to Canadian Dollars:

  1. Assess Transfer Size: For transfers under $25,000 USD, use Wise for instant ACH-to-EFT conversion. For transfers over $25,000, consider OFX or Norbert's Gambit on a Canadian brokerage platform.
  2. Input Canadian Bank Direct Deposit Details: Enter the recipient's 3-digit Financial Institution Number, 5-digit Transit/Branch Number, and account number.
  3. Fund via US ACH Direct Debit: Link your US checking account via Plaid or manual micro-deposits to fund the transfer free of wire charges.
  4. EFT Settlement into Canadian Bank: Funds arrive directly into Canadian chequing accounts (TD, RBC, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC) within 1 to 2 business days.
  5. Comply with FBAR / FINTRAC: Ensure compliance with annual US FBAR (FinCEN 114) reporting if aggregate foreign balances exceed $10,000 USD at any point during the year.

Cross-Border Banking vs Norbert's Gambit for Large Conversions

High-value cross-border movers between the US and Canada have distinct technical execution paths:

🏦 Unified Cross-Border Banking (RBC / TD)

Canadian Big 5 banks operate US retail subsidiaries (RBC Bank US, TD Bank America), allowing single-sign-on instant transfers between linked US checking and Canadian accounts, ideal for snowbirds managing recurring bills.

📈 Norbert's Gambit (0% FX Spread)

For sums exceeding $25,000, Norbert's Gambit involves purchasing Horizons USD ETF (DLR.U.TO) on a Canadian discount brokerage (Questrade, Wealthsimple) and journaling to DLR.TO in CAD, eliminating thousands in foreign exchange spreads.

Tax & Regulatory Compliance Overview

Under US-Canada tax treaties, transferring your own funds between personal US and Canadian accounts is not a taxable event. However, US citizens and Green Card holders in Canada must file FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) if aggregate foreign accounts exceed $10,000 at any point during the year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest way to send money from the US to Canada?
Wise is the most cost-effective method for transfers under $25,000, while OFX or Norbert's Gambit offers lower costs for large sums exceeding $25,000.
How does cross-border banking between US and Canada work?
Banks like RBC, TD, and BMO allow you to open both a US-domiciled bank account and a Canadian account, enabling instant online transfers between your accounts.
What is Norbert's Gambit and when should I use it?
Norbert's Gambit is an investment strategy where you buy a dual-listed ETF in USD and journal it to CAD on a brokerage platform (like Questrade), saving thousands in FX fees on sums over $20,000.
Do I have to pay taxes when transferring money between US and Canada?
No. Simply moving personal funds is not taxable. However, US persons must comply with FBAR reporting if total foreign bank balances exceed $10,000 USD.
How long does an ACH transfer from the US take to reach Canada?
ACH transfers take 1 to 2 business days to clear in the US before being disbursed into Canadian bank accounts via direct electronic funds transfer (EFT).
Can I send money directly to an Interac e-Transfer from the US?
Direct Interac e-Transfers require a Canadian bank account, but digital platforms like Wise allow you to send USD to a Canadian recipient's email linked to Interac Autodeposit.
What is the difference between a USD account in Canada and a US-based account?
A Canadian USD account is held in Canada (subject to Canadian clearing rails), while a US-based account is domiciled in the US with an American routing number and ACH access.
Are US to Canada money transfers reported to FINTRAC?
Yes. Financial institutions in Canada report all international electronic fund transfers of $10,000 CAD or more to FINTRAC within 15 calendar days.
