Independent Editorial Review

Remitly Overview & Background

Founded in 2011 by Matt Oppenheimer, Josh Hug, and Shivaas Gulati, Remitly (NASDAQ: RELY) was created to make cross-border money transfers easier, faster, and more affordable for immigrants sending money home to family members in developing countries. Headquartered in Seattle, Washington, Remitly has grown into one of the largest digital remittance services in the world, processing tens of billions of dollars annually.

Unlike generalist money transfer providers that cater heavily to corporate FX trading, Remitly focuses squarely on personal remittance corridors connecting developed sender nations (like the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Eurozone countries) to developing receiver nations across Asia, Latin America, Africa, and Eastern Europe.

Remitly differentiates itself through flexible delivery channels. Senders can choose between bank account deposits, debit card deposits, home cash delivery, mobile wallet transfers (e.g., M-Pesa, GCash, PayMaya), or physical cash pickup at hundreds of thousands of retail agent locations worldwide.

By leveraging mobile app technology and direct integrations with local clearing networks in receiving countries, Remitly offers far lower transaction fees and much faster delivery speeds than legacy brick-and-mortar money transfer operators like Western Union or MoneyGram.

In addition, Remitly's platform features real-time transfer status tracking via SMS notifications, giving peace of mind to senders and receivers alike.

Remitly's proprietary network architecture connects directly to regional clearing houses, retail bank networks, and digital wallet providers. This bypasses legacy SWIFT correspondent banking chains, reducing middleman deduction fees and eliminating unexpected transfer delays.

Additionally, Remitly provides transparent pricing calculators before booking, allowing customers to view the exact conversion rate, fee breakdown, and recipient payout figure upfront.

Service Offerings

Key Features & Delivery Tiers

Express Tier (Instant Speed)

Funded using a debit or credit card. Money arrives within minutes to the recipient's bank account, card, or cash pickup location. Carries a slightly higher flat fee.

Economy Tier (Low-Cost)

Funded directly from your checking bank account. Takes 3 to 5 business days to clear, but offers zero or heavily discounted transfer fees.

Vast Cash Pickup Network

Partners with major global retail banks and commercial agent locations (such as Elektra, OXXO, Bancomer, Cebuana, and Palawan Express) for instant cash collection.

Exact Delivery Time Guarantee

Remitly provides a precise date and time promise for when funds will arrive. If funds are delayed past this promise time, Remitly refunds your transaction fee.

Cost Structure

Remitly Fee Analysis

Remitly pricing varies depending on three main factors: destination country, funding payment method, and transfer speed tier (Express vs Economy).

  • Economy Fee: Usually $0 USD (free) for transfers over $1,000 USD on major remittance corridors (e.g. US to Philippines, India, or Mexico). For transfers under $1,000, flat fees range from $1.99 to $3.99 USD.
  • Express Fee: Typically $3.99 to $4.99 USD flat fee for instant card-funded transfers.
  • Credit Card Processing Surcharge: Card issuers apply a 3% cash advance fee in addition to Remitly's standard transaction charge.

Understanding Remitly's Variable Margin Model

While Economy transfers carry zero upfront charges on higher amount thresholds, Remitly includes a small currency conversion margin. For instance, if the mid-market rate for USD/INR is 83.50, Remitly might offer an exchange rate of 82.90. This 0.7% spread allows Remitly to maintain zero transfer fees while still covering operating overhead and local settlement costs.

For senders transferring smaller sums (under USD), choosing the Express option funded by a debit card remains the most popular choice because it guarantees delivery within minutes for a nominal flat fee.

FX Margin Policy

Exchange Rate Markups Explained

Unlike Wise, Remitly does not pass through the raw interbank mid-market exchange rate. Instead, Remitly incorporates a currency exchange margin (spread) into the rate offered to customers. This markup typically ranges between 0.5% and 2.0% above the wholesale interbank rate.

However, Remitly frequently runs new-customer promotions offering zero-margin mid-market exchange rates on a user's initial transfer up to a specified cap (e.g. first $500 or $1,000 USD). Even with standard spreads included, Remitly's total transaction cost remains far lower than traditional commercial bank wires.

Compliance Limits

Account Verification & Limits

Remitly operates three distinct sending limit tiers based on the level of customer identity verification completed:

  • Tier 1 (Basic Verification): $2,999 USD per 24 hours / $10,000 USD per 30 days. Requires full legal name, residential address, and date of birth.
  • Tier 2 (ID Verification): $6,000 USD per 24 hours / $20,000 USD per 30 days. Requires government-issued photo ID (passport or driver's license).
  • Tier 3 (Enhanced Verification): $10,000 USD per 24 hours / $30,000 USD per 30 days. Requires proof of address and source of funds documentation.

Compliance & Safety

Security & Regulatory Status

Remitly complies with anti-money laundering (AML) laws and Know Your Customer (KYC) regulations worldwide:

  • United States: Registered as a Money Services Business (MSB) with FinCEN and licensed in all operating states.
  • United Kingdom: Authorized by the FCA under the Payment Services Regulations 2017.
  • Canada: Registered with FINTRAC.
  • Data Protection: Uses 256-bit SSL encryption and Transport Layer Security (TLS) to safeguard customer financial data.

To prevent account takeover and financial fraud, Remitly employs continuous machine-learning risk engines that analyze sender device fingerprints, IP locations, and historical transfer velocity. If unusual activity is detected, automated holds are triggered until the user verifies their identity via multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Furthermore, customer funds are held in segregated accounts with top-tier global banking partners, ensuring user balances remain completely protected and separate from Remitly's corporate operating accounts.

Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Remitly Express and Economy transfers?
Express transfers are funded via debit or credit card and complete within minutes, but carry higher transaction fees. Economy transfers are funded via bank account and take 3 to 5 business days, but offer zero or low flat transfer fees.
Does Remitly support cash pickup?
Yes. Remitly partners with extensive local banks, retail chains, and agent locations worldwide, allowing recipients to pick up physical cash using a reference code and government photo ID.
How does Remitly make money on exchange rates?
Remitly adds an exchange rate margin (spread) to the wholesale mid-market rate. This margin typically ranges from 0.5% to 2.0% depending on currency pair, transfer speed tier, and promotional rate status.
Is Remitly safe and regulated?
Yes. Remitly is registered with FinCEN in the US, licensed as an Authorized Payment Institution by the FCA in the UK, and regulated by ASIC in Australia.
Can I transfer money to mobile wallets via Remitly?
Yes. Remitly supports direct deposits into popular mobile wallets such as M-Pesa, GCash, PayMaya, and bKash in developing markets.
What is Remitly's delivery promise guarantee?
Remitly provides an exact delivery time promise at checkout. If your funds do not arrive by the specified delivery promise time, Remitly refunds your transaction fee.
Are there promotional exchange rates for new Remitly users?
Yes. Remitly frequently offers promotional exchange rates with zero margin or discounted fees on a first transfer for new customers.
What are the transfer limits on Remitly?
Unverified accounts start with basic daily limits around $2,999 USD. By submitting identity verification documents, users can increase their limit to $10,000 USD per day and $30,000 USD per month.
Editorial Conclusion

Final Verdict on Remitly

Remitly is an outstanding option for personal remittances, family financial support, and cash pickup transfers to developing nations. Its dual-tier structure gives senders maximum flexibility: choose Express for instant card-funded delivery, or choose Economy for low-cost bank transfers.

While Wise remains cheaper for bank-to-bank transfers in major currencies (due to Wise's zero-margin FX policy), Remitly's extensive cash pickup agent coverage and mobile wallet integration make it a strong option for sending money to unbanked relatives abroad.
