Independent Editorial Review

Payoneer Overview & Background

Payoneer (NASDAQ: PAYO) was founded in 2005 by Yuval Tal in New York with a visionary goal: building a frictionless global financial bridge for independent workers, digital agencies, and eCommerce merchants operating in the burgeoning international gig economy. Over the past two decades, Payoneer has grown into one of the world's most trusted cross-border B2B financial infrastructures, serving over 5 million active enterprises, marketplaces, and freelancers across 190+ countries.

Payoneer is uniquely engineered for commercial business transactions rather than retail consumer remittances. The core pillar of Payoneer's offering is its Global Payment Service, which provisions users with dedicated local virtual receiving accounts in 9+ major world currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, SGD, CNH, and AED). This allows foreign clients to settle contractor invoices via local domestic clearing rails (such as US ACH, UK Faster Payments, and European SEPA) without incurring expensive international SWIFT wire deductions.

Furthermore, Payoneer serves as the native integrated payment backbone for the world's largest freelance marketplaces and digital platforms—including Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com, Amazon Seller Central, Walmart Marketplace, Airbnb, Getty Images, and Taboola. Freelancers and digital sellers can connect their Payoneer account directly to these platforms to receive automated earnings payouts seamlessly.

Once funds accumulate in a Payoneer multi-currency balance, users can withdraw capital directly to their local domestic bank account in 150+ countries in over 70 local currencies, execute 100% free peer-to-peer transfers to other Payoneer users worldwide, pay international subcontractors, or spend funds globally using the physical or virtual Payoneer Commercial Mastercard.

Pricing Transparency

Payoneer Fee Structure Analyzed

Payoneer operates on a clear business monetization model: receiving payments from other Payoneer users is 100% free. Receiving via local bank details is free in EUR, GBP, and JPY (0% - 1% on USD ACH). Credit card invoice payments incur a 3.0% merchant processing fee.

Bank Withdrawal Spread: When withdrawing foreign currency balances to a local domestic bank account in your home currency, Payoneer applies a currency conversion margin of up to 2.0% above the wholesale interbank rate. Annual account maintenance fee ($29.95 USD) applies only to accounts with zero transaction activity over a 12-month period.

Marketplace Payout Fees: Leading platforms like Upwork and Fiverr charge minimal withdrawal fees ($1.00 to $2.00 USD) to transfer funds into a linked Payoneer wallet, representing a massive cost savings compared to PayPal's 4.4% cross-border fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Payoneer's withdrawal fee to a local bank?
Payoneer charges an exchange rate spread of up to 2.0% above the wholesale interbank rate when you withdraw foreign currency balances to your local domestic bank account.
How is Payoneer cheaper than PayPal for freelancers?
While PayPal charges a 4.4% cross-border fee plus a 3.5% - 4.5% currency markup (totaling ~8% loss), Payoneer charges 0% on receiving via local bank rails and only up to 2% on withdrawal, saving freelancers thousands per year.
What is Payoneer's annual fee?
Payoneer is free to maintain as long as you make at least one transaction every 12 months. If your account has zero activity for a full year, a $29.95 USD annual maintenance fee is deducted.
Can I link my Payoneer receiving account to Upwork and Fiverr?
Yes. Both Upwork and Fiverr have native payment integrations allowing direct, automated earnings withdrawals into your Payoneer balance.
Is Payoneer safe and regulated?
Yes. Payoneer is a publicly traded company on NASDAQ (PAYO) regulated by the US FinCEN, UK FCA, Central Bank of Ireland, and Hong Kong Customs and Excise Department.
What is the Payoneer Commercial Mastercard?
It is a physical or virtual debit card linked to your Payoneer account balance that allows you to make business purchases online, pay for SaaS tools, and withdraw cash at ATMs worldwide.
How long does it take to withdraw money from Payoneer to a bank account?
Withdrawals to local bank accounts typically settle within 1 to 2 business days.
Does Payoneer provide official FIRC/FIRS certificates for tax filing?
Yes. Payoneer automatically generates digital Foreign Inward Remittance Statements (FIRS) for every withdrawal, ensuring compliance with local export tax regulations.
