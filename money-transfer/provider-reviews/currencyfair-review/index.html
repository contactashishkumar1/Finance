Independent Editorial Review

CurrencyFair Overview & Corporate Background

Founded in 2009 in Dublin, Ireland, by Brett Meyers and a team of financial engineers, CurrencyFair pioneered the peer-to-peer (P2P) foreign exchange model for retail and small business currency transfers. In 2021, CurrencyFair merged with Australian financial technology firm Assembly Payments to form Zai Group, creating a unified global payment infrastructure backed by Standard Chartered Bank.

Unlike traditional money transfer companies that act as centralized market makers buying foreign currency at wholesale rates and reselling it with a markup, CurrencyFair operates an open marketplace. On CurrencyFair, users looking to swap Euros for British Pounds can match directly with users looking to swap British Pounds for Euros.

By matching private buyers and sellers directly, CurrencyFair enables senders to achieve exchange rates extremely close to the true mid-market rate—sometimes even beating the interbank rate when marketplace liquidity is high.

For users requiring immediate settlement, CurrencyFair provides an "Auto-Match" option that instantly executes conversions using internal liquidity pools at a competitive standard margin.

With local clearing accounts across Europe, the UK, Australia, Singapore, and Hong Kong, CurrencyFair routes domestic bank wires on both ends, bypassing international correspondent banking networks and keeping fixed transfer costs exceptionally low.

By integrating local payment rails such as SEPA in Europe and Faster Payments in the UK, CurrencyFair eliminates unexpected wire deductions from intermediary correspondent banks, providing total transparency to personal and corporate remitters.

Cost Breakdown

CurrencyFair Fee Structure

CurrencyFair's pricing model consists of two components:

  • Fixed Transfer Fee: €3.00 EUR (or £2.50 GBP / $4.00 AUD / $3.00 USD equivalent) per outbound bank wire transfer.
  • Exchange Rate Margin:
    • P2P Matched Trade: 0.1% to 0.3% margin above mid-market rate.
    • Instant Auto-Match Trade: ~0.45% average margin above mid-market rate.

Because the €3.00 fee is flat, larger transfer amounts benefit significantly from lower percentage overhead, outperforming variable fee structures on large wire transfers.

Marketplace Rate Matching

CurrencyFair Exchange Rate Mechanics

The core innovation of CurrencyFair is its peer-to-peer order book. Senders can post an offer in the marketplace specifying the exact exchange rate they want to achieve.

If another user (or combination of users) in another country wishes to exchange the reverse currency pair at that rate, the trade matches automatically. Because CurrencyFair simply facilitates the trade rather than taking a heavy dealer margin, user-matched rates often beat traditional provider quotes.

The P2P marketplace order book updates dynamically, allowing users to view existing buy and sell orders across popular currency corridors like EUR/GBP, GBP/AUD, and EUR/USD in real time.

Digital App

CurrencyFair Mobile Application

The CurrencyFair mobile application (available on iOS and Android) allows users to manage marketplace exchange orders, execute Auto-Match conversions, monitor rate trends, and initiate bank payouts from anywhere.

Features include push notifications for order matches, biometric login security, multi-currency wallet management, and live chart visualization.

The mobile app also allows users to store beneficiary bank accounts securely, enabling quick repeat transfers to family members, foreign suppliers, or personal overseas bank accounts.

Regulatory Framework

Security, Regulation & Licensing

As part of Zai Group, CurrencyFair adheres to strict international regulatory standards:

  • European Union: Regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland (CBI) under the European Union (Payment Services) Regulations.
  • Australia: Regulated by ASIC and holds an Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL).
  • Singapore: Licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS).
  • Safeguarding & Encryption: All client funds are held in ring-fenced, segregated accounts at top-tier international commercial banks separate from operating funds. Operates 256-bit SSL encryption and strict KYC/AML verification.

CurrencyFair requires mandatory identity verification (KYC) for all personal and business accounts. Users must provide proof of identity (passport or driver's license) and proof of address before booking marketplace trades or executing withdrawals.

Furthermore, two-factor authentication (2FA) is enforced across all customer logins and withdrawal requests, ensuring unauthorized access is prevented even if account passwords are compromised.

Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CurrencyFair's peer-to-peer marketplace work?
CurrencyFair operates a P2P exchange marketplace where individuals and businesses match foreign currency trades with other users seeking opposite currency pairs, eliminating middleman bank markups.
What is CurrencyFair's fixed transfer fee?
CurrencyFair charges a low fixed transfer fee of €3.00 (or equivalent, such as £2.50 / $4.00 AUD) per completed wire transfer out of your multi-currency balance.
What exchange rate margin does CurrencyFair apply?
When matched on the P2P marketplace, exchange margins can be as low as 0.1% to 0.35% above the mid-market rate. For immediate auto-matched transfers, the margin averages around 0.45%.
Can I set my own exchange rate on CurrencyFair?
Yes. CurrencyFair allows users to set a custom exchange rate target in the exchange marketplace and wait for another user to match their trade.
Is CurrencyFair safe and regulated?
Yes. CurrencyFair is a subsidiary of Zai Group and is fully regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland (CBI) under European E-Money Regulations and ASIC in Australia.
How fast are CurrencyFair bank payouts?
Most major currency bank deposits (EUR, GBP, USD) arrive within same-day to 1 to 2 business days after matching.
How does CurrencyFair compare to Wise?
Wise passes through the mid-market rate with a variable percentage fee. CurrencyFair uses a fixed fee (€3) plus P2P matching, making CurrencyFair competitive on medium-to-large transfers.
What happens if my peer-to-peer exchange offer is not matched?
If no user matches your custom rate, you can adjust your target rate closer to the current market or select Auto-Match for immediate conversion.
Does CurrencyFair support multi-currency balances?
Yes. Senders can deposit, hold, and manage balances in over 20 global currencies inside their CurrencyFair account.
Does CurrencyFair support business accounts?
Yes. CurrencyFair for Business enables international supplier payouts, global client invoicing, and multi-user administrative access.
Can I fund CurrencyFair transfers with a credit card?
In select European markets, debit card funding is supported; however, CurrencyFair primarily relies on local bank transfers to minimize costs.
Does CurrencyFair support physical cash pickup?
No. CurrencyFair operates exclusively as a digital bank-to-bank transfer platform and does not offer physical cash collection.
Are customer balances safeguarded with CurrencyFair?
Yes. In compliance with Central Bank of Ireland regulations, client funds are held in ring-fenced, segregated accounts at top-tier European commercial banks.
What are the transfer limits on CurrencyFair?
CurrencyFair imposes no hard upper limit on online transfer amounts once full account identity verification is completed.
Which countries can use CurrencyFair?
CurrencyFair is available to residents across the European Union, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong, and select global regions.
Editorial Conclusion

Final Verdict on CurrencyFair

CurrencyFair remains an innovative and cost-effective solution for cross-border bank wire transfers, particularly across European and Asia-Pacific currency corridors. Its peer-to-peer marketplace model gives users rare control over exchange rate target setting.

While P2P trade matching can require additional patience compared to instant brokers, the combination of a flat €3.00 wire fee and ultra-tight exchange rate spreads makes CurrencyFair highly competitive for medium-to-large international transfers.
