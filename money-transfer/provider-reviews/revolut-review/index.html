Revolut Overview & Background

Revolut was founded in July 2015 at London's Level39 technology accelerator by Nikolay Storonsky (a former Credit Suisse foreign exchange trader) and Vlad Yatsenko (a former software architect at Deutsche Bank). Frustrated by the excessive foreign exchange markups, hidden wire fees, and slow correspondent banking settlement cycles imposed by legacy high-street banks, the founders designed Revolut to pass through raw interbank wholesale foreign exchange rates directly to global consumers.

What began as a multi-currency prepaid travel card has evolved into a global banking powerhouse serving over 45 million personal and business account holders across the United Kingdom, European Economic Area (EEA), United States, Singapore, Japan, Australia, and New Zealand. Revolut processes over 400 million transactions per month, representing tens of billions of dollars in cross-border trade, remittances, and everyday card payments.

Revolut operates on a distinctive freemium subscription architecture—offering free Standard accounts alongside feature-enhanced Plus, Premium, Metal, and Ultra subscription tiers. During standard global financial market hours (Monday through Friday), Revolut provides live interbank exchange rates up to designated monthly plan allowances with zero added currency margin.

In addition to peer-to-peer international money transfers, Revolut functions as a complete digital financial hub. Features include multi-currency local IBAN routing details, disposable virtual cards for bulletproof online merchant security, high-yield savings Vaults, automated fractional investing in global equities and commodities, international travel medical insurance, and integrated corporate expense management for businesses.

Pricing Transparency

Revolut Fee Structure Analyzed

Revolut operates on a transparent, two-pillar fee model that differentiates between standard weekday market hours and weekend market closures. On the free Standard account tier, customers enjoy a generous monthly fee-free currency exchange allowance of £1,000 GBP / €1,000 EUR / $1,000 USD per rolling 30-day billing cycle. Once a user exceeds this threshold within the monthly cycle, Revolut applies a modest 1.0% fair usage fee (0.5% on Plus plans, and 0% on Premium, Metal, and Ultra tiers).

The Weekend Currency Surcharge Explained: When foreign exchange markets close on Friday evening (5:00 PM EST) through Sunday evening (6:00 PM EST), global interbank liquidity providers cease quoting live spot prices. To safeguard against unexpected gap risk and Monday morning market opening volatility, Revolut automatically adds a 1.0% fee markup to all currency exchanges and cross-border transfers booked during the weekend window across major currency pairs.

International Outbound Bank Wire Fees: Sending money to other Revolut users worldwide is 100% free and instant regardless of currency. For outward bank transfers, domestic payments in local currency (e.g. SEPA in Europe or Faster Payments in the UK) are completely free. For cross-border wires sent outside local payment zones, standard users receive discounted wire pricing that is transparently calculated and displayed in the app before booking.

FX Margin Policy

Exchange Rate Markups Explained

Revolut determines exchange rates based on live interbank foreign exchange market feeds. These are the same interbank benchmark rates institutional currency dealers and commercial banks trade amongst themselves, devoid of retail dealer markups on weekdays. Senders can view live exchange charts directly inside the mobile app and configure Auto-Exchange triggers—allowing the app to automatically convert balances the millisecond a target exchange rate threshold is breached.

Pro Tip: Compare the rate quoted by Revolut against the live Google mid-market benchmark before confirming. Use our Exchange Rate Markup Calculator to calculate the exact percentage fee embedded in the currency conversion.

Regulatory Protection

Security, Licensing & Fund Safety

Security and regulatory compliance are top priorities for Revolut. In the European Union, Revolut operates as a licensed commercial credit institution under European Central Bank (ECB) authorization via the Bank of Lithuania, ensuring eligible customer deposits are protected up to €100,000 under the statutory European Deposit Guarantee Scheme. In the United Kingdom, Revolut operates as an Electronic Money Institution (EMI) authorized by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), safeguarding 100% of customer funds in segregated trust accounts with tier-1 global banks (Barclays, Lloyds). In the United States, banking services are provided through FDIC-insured partner institutions (Metropolitan Commercial Bank).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Revolut's weekend exchange fee?
Revolut charges a 1.0% fee surcharge on all currency conversions executed between Friday 5:00 PM EST and Sunday 6:00 PM EST to hedge against market gap risk while global FX markets are closed.
Is money held in Revolut safe and insured?
In the European Union, deposits are protected up to €100,000 under the Lithuanian Deposit Insurance scheme. In the UK and US, funds are safeguarded in segregated accounts at regulated tier-1 partner banks.
How much can I exchange for free on Revolut's standard plan?
On the free Standard plan, users can exchange up to £1,000 / €1,000 / $1,000 per rolling month at interbank rates. Exchanges exceeding this cap incur a 1.0% fair usage fee.
How fast are Revolut international money transfers?
Transfers between Revolut accounts are instant. Outbound bank transfers to SEPA countries take minutes, while SWIFT transfers to Asia or North America take 1 to 2 business days.
Can I use Revolut to receive client payments as a freelancer?
Yes. Revolut provides multi-currency local IBAN and routing numbers, and also offers Revolut Pro and Revolut Business accounts for freelance invoicing.
Does Revolut support cash pickup delivery?
No. Revolut only supports transfers to bank accounts, cards, and other Revolut mobile users.
What are the advantages of Revolut Metal or Premium plans?
Paid plans remove the monthly fee-free exchange cap, provide higher free ATM limits, include global medical travel insurance, and offer discounted international transfer fees.
How do I avoid Revolut transfer fees?
Execute your currency conversions Monday through Friday during market hours, and keep conversions within your monthly plan allowance.
