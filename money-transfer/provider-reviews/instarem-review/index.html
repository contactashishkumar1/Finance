Independent Editorial Review

Instarem Overview & Corporate Background

Founded in 2014 in Singapore by Prajit Nanu and Michael Bermingham, Instarem (originally short for "Instant Remittance") was established to eliminate high bank markups on cross-border payments across Southeast and South Asia. Instarem evolved into Nium Pte. Ltd., a global B2B financial infrastructure unicorn valued at over $2 billion, operating consumer and commercial remittance services worldwide.

Powered by Nium's institutional global clearing network, Instarem connects directly into real-time payment systems across Asia, including India's UPI/IMPS, Singapore's PayNow, Malaysia's DuitNow, and the Philippines' InstaPay.

Instarem sets itself apart through transparent FX pricing. On major currency corridors, Instarem passes through rates very close to the mid-market rate while charging a clear upfront transaction percentage fee.

In addition to money transfers, Instarem introduced the Amaze Card ecosystem in markets like Singapore. The Amaze Card allows users to link multiple existing bank credit cards, process international transactions at transparent FX rates, and earn additional rewards points (InstaPoints).

Nium's global treasury infrastructure enables Instarem to maintain pre-funded accounts with local clearing banks in over 40 countries. This local payout mechanism bypasses the costly legacy SWIFT banking chain, ensuring near-instant delivery times and eliminating unexpected middleman bank deductions.

Deep-Dive: How the Amaze Card System Optimizes Travel Spending

One of Instarem's most unique product offerings is the Amaze Card, currently available to residents in Singapore. Rather than requiring users to top up a prepaid balance (like typical multi-currency travel cards), Amaze acts as a virtual aggregator card.

Users link up to five existing Mastercard credit or debit cards inside the Instarem app. When tapping the Amaze Card at an overseas merchant or e-commerce store, the transaction is billed directly to the designated linked credit card in local currency, while Instarem handles the foreign exchange conversion at wholesale rates.

This allows cardholders to earn standard credit card rewards from their issuing bank while avoiding the traditional 3.25% to 3.5% foreign currency transaction surcharges charged by traditional retail banks.

Cost Breakdown

Instarem Fee Structure

Instarem's fee model is designed to be fully transparent:

  • Upfront Transfer Fee: Ranging from 0.25% to 0.6% of the transfer amount depending on the currency pair.
  • FX Spread: Instarem applies minimal-to-zero exchange rate markups on major corridors (such as USD/INR, SGD/INR, AUD/PHP), relying on upfront fees for monetization.

This transparent separation of transfer charges guarantees that senders see the exact cost before confirming a transfer, with zero hidden fees subtracted upon delivery.

Regulatory Framework

Security, Regulation & Compliance

As part of Nium, Instarem is regulated by tier-1 global monetary authorities:

  • Singapore: Licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) as a Major Payment Institution.
  • United Kingdom: Authorized by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA).
  • United States: Registered with FinCEN and licensed state-by-state.
  • Australia: Regulated by ASIC and holding an AFSL license.
  • Safeguarding & Encryption: Operates 256-bit SSL encryption, holds client funds in segregated safeguard accounts at major global banks, and enforces strict KYC/AML identity verification.

Instarem adheres to global Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards, conducting real-time transaction screening and mandatory identity verification to ensure all cross-border funds comply with international banking mandates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What company owns Instarem?
Instarem is the consumer and small business remittance brand of Nium Pte. Ltd., a global fintech infrastructure platform licensed across major financial jurisdictions worldwide.
What is the Amaze Card ecosystem?
The Amaze Card is a smart travel card issued by Instarem (in select markets like Singapore) that links existing credit/debit cards, converting currencies at transparent FX rates while earning rewards.
Does Instarem charge transfer fees?
Instarem charges a transparent upfront percentage fee (typically between 0.25% and 0.6%) with zero hidden exchange rate markups on major corridors.
How fast are Instarem transfers to Asian bank accounts?
Transfers to major Asian markets (India, Philippines, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia) deliver within minutes to same-day via Nium's direct clearing connections.
What is the InstaPoints loyalty program?
InstaPoints is Instarem's customer rewards program where senders earn points on every transfer that can be redeemed for transaction discounts or cash back.
How does Instarem compare to Wise for transfers to Asia?
Instarem offers competitive transparent rates matching Wise, while providing regional specializations across Southeast and South Asia along with the Amaze Card ecosystem.
Is Instarem safe and regulated?
Yes. Instarem (Nium) is authorized and regulated by tier-1 authorities including MAS in Singapore, FCA in the UK, FinCEN in the US, and ASIC in Australia.
Can businesses use Instarem for international payments?
Yes. Instarem for Business allows SMEs to execute international mass payouts, settle vendor invoices, and manage multi-currency payroll.
What payment methods can I use to fund an Instarem transfer?
Senders can fund transfers via electronic bank debit (ACH / EFT / PayNow / Fast), bank wire, or debit cards.
Does Instarem support cash pickup?
Instarem primarily focuses on direct bank deposits and digital wallet payouts (such as GCash or GrabPay), with physical cash pickup available in select Asian corridors.
Are client funds safe with Instarem?
Yes. Client funds are ring-fenced in segregated safeguard accounts with licensed institutional banks in accordance with international regulatory requirements.
What are the transfer limits on Instarem?
Online transfer limits depend on verification tier and funding method, with verified accounts sending up to $50,000 USD (or local equivalent) per transaction.
Which countries can register for Instarem?
Instarem accepts sender registrations from the US, UK, Eurozone, Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong, India, Malaysia, and Canada.
Can I cancel an Instarem transfer?
If the transfer has not been processed by Nium's clearing system, users can request cancellation through the app or customer service desk.
How does Instarem show exchange rates before booking?
Instarem displays the exact exchange rate and breakdown of upfront fees prior to transfer confirmation, guaranteeing payout amounts.
