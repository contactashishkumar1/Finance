Independent Editorial Review

OFX Overview & Corporate Background

Founded in 1998 in Sydney, Australia, as OzForex, OFX (OFX Group Limited, ASX: OFX) is an international foreign exchange provider listed publicly on the Australian Securities Exchange. Headquartered in Sydney with major global hubs in London, San Francisco, Toronto, Auckland, and Singapore, OFX has transferred over $150 billion in volume for individuals and corporate clients across more than 50 currencies.

OFX occupies a unique market niche bridging digital payment platforms and full-service institutional forex brokerages. While many fintech competitors rely exclusively on automated self-service apps, OFX combines digital web/app booking with 24/7 phone access to dedicated human currency brokers ("OFX Personal Dealers").

This hybrid operational model makes OFX particularly attractive to individuals transferring substantial sums—such as purchasing overseas real estate, transferring foreign inheritances, emigrating abroad, or paying international tuition—where having a knowledgeable human specialist verify wire instructions provides critical peace of mind.

Additionally, OFX offers robust corporate foreign exchange solutions, allowing businesses to execute multi-currency vendor payments, manage global payroll, and lock in exchange rates up to 12 months in advance using forward contracts.

By establishing local banking clearing accounts in over 50 countries, OFX routes funds domestically on both sides of a cross-border transaction. This localized clearing model eliminates correspondent bank intermediary fees and speeds up wire settlement times.

Cost Structure

OFX Fee Analysis

OFX operates a $0 upfront transfer fee policy on international money transfers across major routes. Senders registering through ArthCalculator incur no fixed transaction fees regardless of the transfer size.

Because OFX does not charge upfront transaction fees, its primary revenue stream comes from a small exchange rate margin (spread) added to the interbank currency rate.

Unlike traditional retail banks that charge $30 to $50 in wire fees plus 3% to 5% FX markups, OFX delivers significant cost savings on transfers exceeding $5,000.

FX Margin Policy

OFX Exchange Rate Markups

OFX applies a tiered exchange rate markup model above the interbank mid-market rate. As the transfer sum increases, the percentage markup decreases significantly:

  • Standard Transfers ($2,000 - $10,000): Spreads range between 0.7% and 1.0%.
  • Large Transfers ($10,000 - $50,000): Spreads narrow to approximately 0.5%.
  • Institutional / High Net Worth ($100,000+): Custom rate spreads negotiable directly with an OFX Personal Dealer, often dropping below 0.4%.

For high-value transfers, OFX's telephone dealers can provide real-time market updates and advise senders on market volatility before locking in a binding rate contract.

Regulatory Status

Security, Compliance & Licensing

OFX is subject to stringent financial regulations globally:

  • Australia: Regulated by ASIC (Australian Securities and Investments Commission) and holds an AFSL license. Listed publicly on the ASX (ASX: OFX).
  • United States: Registered with FinCEN as a Money Services Business and licensed state-by-state.
  • United Kingdom: Authorized by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) as an Electronic Money Institution.
  • Safeguarding & Encryption: Operates 256-bit TLS encryption and holds customer funds in segregated safeguard accounts with tier-1 international banks separate from OFX corporate capital.

OFX enforces comprehensive Anti-Money Laundering (AML) monitoring and Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification policies. Account registration requires submitting verified proof of address and official government photo identification, ensuring full compliance with international banking security mandates.

Additionally, OFX's corporate public listing on the Australian Securities Exchange subjects the firm to rigorous statutory audit requirements, financial disclosure standards, and capital reserve obligations.

Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Does OFX charge transfer fees?
No. OFX charges zero upfront transfer fees on international money transfers through ArthCalculator across major currency corridors.
What is OFX's exchange rate markup?
OFX applies a small margin to the interbank mid-market exchange rate. This markup typically ranges between 0.4% and 1.0%, with tighter margins offered on larger transfer sums.
What is OFX Personal Dealer support?
OFX provides 24/7 access to dedicated human currency specialists over the phone, allowing clients to discuss market timing, negotiate rates, and set up complex hedging orders.
What is the minimum transfer amount on OFX?
OFX requires a minimum single transfer amount of $2,000 USD (or equivalent) in most operating regions.
Can I lock in an exchange rate for a future transfer with OFX?
Yes. OFX offers Forward Contracts, allowing senders to fix an exchange rate today for a transfer occurring up to 12 months in the future.
How does OFX compare to Wise for high-value transfers?
While Wise passes through mid-market rates with a variable percentage fee, OFX charges zero upfront fees and provides dedicated phone broker assistance, making OFX preferable for large property or corporate transfers.
Is OFX regulated and safe?
Yes. OFX (OFX Group Limited, ASX: OFX) is publicly listed on the Australian Securities Exchange and regulated by FinCEN in the US, FCA in the UK, and ASIC in Australia.
How fast are OFX bank wire transfers?
Most OFX major currency transfers settle within 1 to 2 business days after funds reach OFX's clearing bank.
What is an OFX Limit Order?
A Limit Order allows you to set a desired exchange rate target. OFX automatically executes your money transfer if market rates reach your chosen level 24/7.
Does OFX support business and corporate foreign exchange?
Yes. OFX Business offers global supplier payments, multi-currency accounts, batch payments, and customized FX risk management for corporate accounts.
Does OFX support cash pickup?
No. OFX specializes strictly in electronic bank-to-bank wire transfers and does not support cash pickup or mobile wallet payouts.
Can I use OFX to buy overseas real estate?
Yes. OFX is widely utilized by individuals purchasing foreign properties due to its high transfer limits, zero transfer fees, and phone broker support.
How do I fund an OFX transfer?
Transfers can be funded via electronic bank debit (ACH / EFT) or domestic wire transfer directly to OFX's local client account.
Are client funds safe with OFX?
Yes. OFX holds client funds in segregated bank accounts separate from corporate operating capital, complying with strict global safeguarding regulations.
Can I cancel an OFX transfer after booking?
Once a rate is locked in, an OFX transfer is a binding contract. Cancellation may incur market loss charges if exchange rates move unfavorably.
