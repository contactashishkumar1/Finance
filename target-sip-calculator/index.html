Target Amount SIP — Complete Goal Planning Guide

What is Target Amount SIP?

Target Amount SIP planning (reverse SIP calculation) determines how much you need to invest every month to reach a specific financial goal—such as ₹1 Crore for retirement, ₹50 Lakhs for a child's higher education, or ₹25 Lakhs for a home down payment.

Why Use Goal-Based SIP?

Investing without a target often leads to under-investing. By calculating the exact monthly commitment required, you can align your monthly budget, track actual portfolio performance against your target, and increase your SIP when income rises.

💡 Pro Hack: Step-Up SIP

Increasing your SIP contribution by 10% annually reduces your required starting monthly investment by up to 40%. It aligns your SIP with annual salary hikes and dramatically accelerates goal achievement.

⚡ Importance of Inflation

Always adjust your target goal for inflation (typically 6% in India). A ₹1 Crore goal today will require ₹2.4 Crore after 15 years to maintain the exact same purchasing power.

🛡️ De-risking Near Goal

When you are 2–3 years away from your target date, gradually shift your equity corpus into liquid or short-term debt funds to lock in your gains and protect against market drawdowns.

⚠️ 4 Common Mistakes in Target SIP Planning

  • • Assuming Unrealistic Return Rates: Expecting 18–20% annual returns leads to severe SIP underfunding. Stick to realistic 12–14% equity returns.
  • • Ignoring Tax Deductions on Redemption: Equity long-term capital gains (LTCG) above ₹1.25 Lakhs per year attract 12.5% tax. Plan your withdrawal strategy to minimize tax.
  • • Pausing SIPs During Market Falls: Market corrections allow you to buy more mutual fund units at lower NAVs, which accelerates reaching your target corpus.
  • • Not Accounting for Expense Ratios: Direct mutual fund plans save 0.5%–1% annually compared to regular plans, adding lakhs to your final corpus.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much SIP is needed to make ₹1 Crore in 10 years?
Assuming an expected return of 12% p.a., you need a monthly SIP of approximately ₹43,040 to accumulate ₹1 Crore in 10 years. At 15% expected return, the required monthly SIP reduces to around ₹36,150.
What is a Target Amount SIP Calculator?
Unlike a standard SIP calculator that calculates future value from a given monthly investment, a Target Amount SIP calculator works in reverse: it calculates the exact monthly investment required to reach a specific financial goal.
Should I adjust my target goal for inflation?
Yes! Inflation erodes purchasing power over time. ₹1 Crore after 15 years at 6% annual inflation will have the purchasing power of only about ₹41.7 Lakhs today. Adjusting for inflation ensures your target goal meets real future expenses.
What return rate should I assume for equity SIP?
For Indian equity mutual funds over a 7-10+ year horizon, financial advisors generally suggest assuming a conservative 12% to 14% CAGR. For hybrid or debt funds, assume 7% to 9%.
What if I increase my SIP annually (Step-up SIP)?
Increasing your SIP by even 10% every year reduces your required starting monthly investment significantly. For example, to reach ₹1 Cr in 15 years at 12% return, a flat SIP requires ₹20,000/mo, whereas a 10% step-up SIP requires a starting investment of only ~₹11,500/mo.
What is the formula used in Target SIP calculation?
The formula used is: Monthly SIP = Target Amount / [{(1 + r)^n - 1} / r * (1 + r)], where 'r' is monthly interest rate (Annual Return / 12 / 100) and 'n' is total months (Years * 12).
Can I reach my target faster by investing lump sum + SIP?
Yes! If you already have an existing lump sum corpus, its compounded growth reduces the remaining balance needed from your monthly SIP, lowering your required monthly investment.
How often should I review my target SIP plan?
You should review your target SIP portfolio annually to rebalance assets, adjust for actual mutual fund CAGR, and increase contributions in line with annual salary increments.
