Section 80EEA Affordable Housing Tax Guide

How Sec 24(b) + Sec 80EEA Work Together

Section 24(b) allows up to ₹2,00,000 deduction on home loan interest for self-occupied properties. Section 80EEA allows an extra ₹1,50,000 deduction, bringing the total home loan interest tax deduction to ₹3,50,000 per year under the Old Tax Regime.

Four Key Eligibility Conditions

1. Stamp duty value of property ≤ ₹45 Lakhs.
2. First-time homebuyer on loan sanction date.
3. Loan sanctioned between April 1, 2019 and March 31, 2022.
4. Carpet area ≤ 60 sq.m (metros) or 90 sq.m (non-metros).

💡 Pro Hack: Joint Home Loan

If husband and wife buy an affordable home jointly, BOTH co-borrowers can claim ₹2L (Sec 24b) + ₹1.5L (Sec 80EEA) = ₹3.5 Lakhs EACH, creating a massive ₹7 Lakhs total annual tax deduction.

⚡ Add Sec 80C Principal

In addition to ₹3.5 Lakhs interest deduction, you can claim up to ₹1.5 Lakhs principal repayment under Section 80C, taking total home loan tax deduction to ₹5 Lakhs per year!

🛡️ Stamp Value vs Market Value

The ₹45 Lakh cap applies to the Stamp Duty Value recorded by the registrar, not the total market agreement value (which may include amenities or parking).

⚠️ 3 Common Section 80EEA Mistakes

  • • Claiming 80EEA Under New Tax Regime: Section 80EEA is NOT allowed under the New Tax Regime. File under the Old Tax Regime to claim this benefit.
  • • Claiming Both Section 80EE and 80EEA: You cannot claim Section 80EEA if you are already claiming Section 80EE for an earlier loan.
  • • Exceeding ₹45 Lakh Stamp Duty Value: If the official stamp duty valuation is ₹45.5 Lakhs, the entire 80EEA benefit is forfeited.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 80EEA of the Income Tax Act?
Section 80EEA allows first-time homebuyers purchasing affordable residential property to claim an ADDITIONAL tax deduction of up to ₹1,50,000 on home loan interest, over and above the ₹2,00,000 limit under Section 24(b) (total ₹3.5 Lakhs deduction).
What are the eligibility conditions for Section 80EEA?
1. Stamp duty value of the property must not exceed ₹45 Lakhs.
2. Buyer must be a first-time homebuyer (owning no other residential property on loan sanction date).
3. Loan sanctioned between April 1, 2019 and March 31, 2022.
4. Buyer cannot claim Section 80EE.
How does Section 80EEA combine with Section 24(b)?
First claim up to ₹2,00,000 under Section 24(b). Any remaining interest paid above ₹2 Lakhs can be claimed under Section 80EEA up to a maximum of ₹1,50,000.
What is the maximum carpet area limit for Section 80EEA?
Carpet area must not exceed 60 sq. meters (645 sq. ft.) in metropolitan cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) or 90 sq. meters (968 sq. ft.) in non-metropolitan cities.
Is Section 80EEA available under the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80EEA deduction is available only under the Old Tax Regime. The New Tax Regime does not allow 80EEA deductions.
Can joint owners claim Section 80EEA separately?
Yes! If both husband and wife are joint owners and co-applicants on the loan, BOTH can claim up to ₹2 Lakhs (Sec 24b) + ₹1.5 Lakhs (Sec 80EEA) = ₹3.5 Lakhs EACH, creating ₹7 Lakhs total household tax deduction.
Can I claim Section 80EEA if I already own a plot of land?
Yes! Owning a vacant plot of land or commercial property does not disqualify you. You must not own any other 'residential house' on the date of loan sanction.
How many years can Section 80EEA be claimed?
You can claim Section 80EEA every financial year for the entire active repayment tenure of your home loan.
