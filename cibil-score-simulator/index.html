How CIBIL Credit Scores are Calculated in India

TransUnion CIBIL evaluates creditworthiness on a scale of 300 to 900 based on credit bureau reports submitted monthly by Indian banks and NBFCs.

CIBIL Score Rating Slabs (2026)

Score Range Category Impact on Loan Approval & Interest Rates
750 - 900 Excellent Instant approval at lowest interest rates
700 - 749 Good High approval chances with standard interest rates
650 - 699 Fair Possible approval with higher interest & collateral
300 - 649 Poor High risk of loan rejection

Key Factors Driving Your CIBIL Credit Score (300-900 Scale)

  • Payment History (35% Weight): Timely EMI and credit card bill payments are the single biggest driver of a 750+ CIBIL score.
  • Credit Utilization Ratio (30% Weight): Keep credit card spend below 30% of your total assigned credit limit.
  • Credit Mix & History Length (25% Weight): A balanced mix of secured (home/car) and unsecured (personal/credit card) loans over long tenure.

Actionable Steps to Rebound from 600 to 750+ CIBIL

  • Zero Default Guarantee: Set up auto-debit for all credit card total amounts due (never pay minimum due only).
  • Limit Hard Enquiries: Avoid applying for multiple credit cards or personal loans within short timeframes (max 1 enquiry per quarter).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CIBIL score in India?
A CIBIL score of 750 or above (out of 900) is considered excellent by Indian banks and financial institutions for loan approval.
What factors impact your CIBIL score the most?
Payment History accounts for 35%, Credit Utilization Ratio accounts for 30%, Credit Age accounts for 15%, and Credit Mix & Hard Inquiries account for 20%.
How much does a missed EMI payment lower CIBIL score?
A single 30+ day missed EMI or credit card payment can drop your CIBIL score by 50 to 80 points.
What is the recommended credit utilization ratio?
Keeping your total credit utilization ratio below 30% of your total credit limit helps maintain a high credit score.
How long does it take to rebuild a low CIBIL score?
With disciplined on-time payments and low credit utilization, a low score can improve by 50-100 points within 6 to 12 months.
Does checking your own CIBIL score lower it?
No, checking your own credit score is a 'Soft Inquiry' and has zero negative impact on your CIBIL score.
What is a Hard Inquiry?
A Hard Inquiry occurs when a bank or credit card company checks your credit report during a loan application. Multiple hard inquiries in a short time lower your score.
How does closing an old credit card affect CIBIL?
Closing your oldest credit card reduces your total credit history length and increases credit utilization, which can temporarily lower your score.
