Gift Tax Provisions Under Section 56(2)(x)

Defined Relatives (100% Tax Free)

Gifts from specified relatives are 100% tax-free without any monetary capping limit. Specified relatives include:

• Spouse | • Parents & Grandparents | • Children & Grandchildren | • Siblings (and their spouses) | • In-laws (Spouse's siblings & parents)

Non-Relative ₹50,000 Cliff Threshold

If total monetary/property gifts from non-relatives (friends, colleagues) are up to ₹50,000 per year, tax is ₹0. However, if total non-relative gifts reach ₹50,001, the ENTIRE ₹50,001 becomes taxable at your income slab rate.

💡 Pro Hack: Marriage Gifts

All cash and property gifts received by the bride or groom on the occasion of their marriage (from anyone, including friends) are 100% tax-free with zero upper limit.

⚡ Clubbing of Income Alert

Gifting money to your spouse or minor child is tax-free. However, any interest or capital gains generated by investing that money will be clubbed back and taxed in your hands under Section 64.

🛡️ Execute a Gift Deed

For high-value cash transfers or property gifts between relatives, execute a formal Gift Deed on stamp paper to prevent income tax queries during ITR assessment.

⚠️ 3 Common Gift Tax Mistakes

  • • Assuming Cousin or Uncle is a 'Relative': Income tax law does NOT include cousins, nephews, or distant uncles in the relative definition. Gifts from them above ₹50,000 are taxable.
  • • Thinking ₹50,000 Limit is Per Gift: The ₹50,000 threshold applies to the AGGREGATE sum of all non-relative gifts received throughout the financial year.
  • • Failing to Declare Non-Relative Gifts in ITR: Omitting non-relative gifts above ₹50,000 from 'Schedule OS' in your ITR can trigger automated tax notices for unexplained cash deposits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is defined as a 'Relative' for tax-free gifts in India?
Under Section 56(2)(x), 'Relatives' include: Spouse, Brother or Sister (and their spouses), Brother or Sister of spouse, Lineal Ascendants/Descendants (Parents, Grandparents, Children, Grandchildren, and their spouses).
Are gifts from friends taxable in India?
Friends do not fall under the definition of 'Relative'. If total monetary or property gifts received from friends exceed ₹50,000 in a financial year, the ENTIRE gift amount is fully taxable under 'Income from Other Sources'.
What is the ₹50,000 threshold rule for gift tax?
If total non-relative gifts received in a financial year are up to ₹50,000, zero tax is payable. However, if total non-relative gifts cross ₹50,001, the entire amount (not just the excess) becomes taxable.
Are wedding gifts tax-exempt in India?
Yes! Gifts received on the occasion of marriage of the individual (from anyone, including friends) are 100% tax-free without any upper limit under Section 56(2)(x).
Are gifts received under a Will or Inheritance taxable?
No. Any money or property received under a Will or by way of inheritance is 100% exempt from income tax in India.
How is immovable property (land/flat) gifted taxed?
If real estate is received without consideration from a non-relative and its stamp duty value exceeds ₹50,000, the stamp duty value is added to your taxable income.
Does clubbing of income apply to gifts given to a spouse or minor child?
Yes! Under Section 64, while the initial gift to spouse/minor child is tax-free, any future income generated from that gifted asset (e.g. interest or rent) will be clubbed back and taxed in the giver's hands.
Is a formal Gift Deed required for gifting cash or property?
A registered Gift Deed (with stamp duty) is mandatory for gifting immovable property. For high-value cash or gold gifts between relatives, executing a simple notarized Gift Deed on stamp paper is strongly advised for ITR audit proof.
