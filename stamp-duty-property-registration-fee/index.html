Property Stamp Duty & Registration Guide

Circle Rate vs Agreement Value

Stamp duty is always levied on whichever value is HIGHER between the agreed purchase price and the government's minimum circle rate for that locality.

Women Buyer Concessions

Registering property in a female family member's name (solely or jointly) unlocks a 1% to 2% stamp duty discount in states like Delhi, UP, and Haryana, saving ₹50,000 to ₹2,000,000!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stamp Duty in real estate?
Stamp Duty is a mandatory tax levied by state governments in India when transferring property ownership to legally register the sale deed in government land records.
How is Stamp Duty calculated?
Stamp Duty is calculated as a percentage of the HIGHER of two values: 1. Agreed Property Agreement Value or 2. Government Circle Rate (Ready Reckoner Rate).
Do female buyers get a discount on Stamp Duty in India?
Yes! Many Indian states (Delhi, UP, Haryana, Punjab, Maharashtra) offer a 1% to 2% stamp duty discount when property is registered solely or jointly in a woman's name.
What is Property Registration Fee?
Registration Fee is the fee paid to the sub-registrar office to record the property deed. It is typically 1% of the property value (or capped at ₹30,000 in states like Maharashtra).
Is Stamp Duty tax-deductible under Section 80C?
Yes! Stamp duty and registration fees paid for purchasing a residential house can be claimed as a tax deduction under Section 80C up to the ₹1.5 Lakh annual limit (Old Tax Regime).
Can Stamp Duty be included in a Home Loan?
Per RBI rules, banks cannot include stamp duty and registration fees in the official home loan LTV (Loan-to-Value) calculation. Buyers must fund stamp duty out-of-pocket.
What happens if Stamp Duty is underpaid?
Underpaying stamp duty makes the property sale deed legally invalid in a court of law. State authorities levy heavy penalty fines (up to 2% per month on unpaid duty).
What is the difference between Agreement Value and Circle Rate?
Agreement Value is the actual price agreed between buyer and seller. Circle Rate is the minimum valuation fixed by the state government for a specific area.
