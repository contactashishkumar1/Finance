Key Features of Section 80EEB

Section 80EEB was introduced to promote green mobility. Individual taxpayers can claim up to ₹1,50,000 deduction on electric 2-wheeler and 4-wheeler loan interest every year until the loan is paid off.

Effective Interest Rate Subsidization

For a taxpayer in the 30% tax bracket, claiming 80EEB reduces an effective 9.5% EV loan interest rate down to just ~6.5% after tax savings!

💡 Pro Hack: Joint EV Loan Benefit

If an EV loan is taken jointly with a working spouse/parent, both co-borrowers can claim up to ₹1.5 Lakhs EACH (total ₹3.0 Lakhs family tax deduction)!

⚡ Business EV Additional Benefit

Self-employed professionals can also claim annual vehicle depreciation (40% for EVs) under business expenses on top of 80EEB interest deduction.

🛡️ Annual Bank Interest Certificate

Obtain a Form 16 / Interest Certificate from your bank at the end of each financial year highlighting exact interest paid on the EV loan.

⚠️ 3 Common Sec 80EEB Mistakes

  • • Claiming in New Tax Regime: 80EEB is NOT allowed in New Tax Regime (Sec 115BAC). You must switch to Old Tax Regime to claim it.
  • • Trying to Claim Hybrid / Petrol Cars: Section 80EEB strictly requires 100% Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs). Plug-in Hybrids do not qualify.
  • • Including Principal Repayment: Only loan interest is deductible under 80EEB; principal repayment cannot be claimed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 80EEB of the Income Tax Act?
Section 80EEB allows individual taxpayers to claim an income tax deduction of up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year for interest paid on loans taken to purchase an Electric Vehicle (EV).
What vehicles qualify under Section 80EEB?
Both 2-wheeler electric scooters/bikes and 4-wheeler electric cars purchased for personal or business use qualify under Section 80EEB.
What was the loan sanction window for Section 80EEB eligibility?
To claim 80EEB deduction, the electric vehicle loan must have been sanctioned by a bank or NBFC between April 1, 2019, and March 31, 2023. Interest on sanctioned loans can be claimed continuously until the loan is fully repaid!
Is Section 80EEB available in both Old and New Tax Regimes?
No. Section 80EEB deduction is ONLY available under the OLD Tax Regime. It cannot be claimed under the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC).
Can Hindu Undivided Families (HUF) or Companies claim Section 80EEB?
No. Section 80EEB deduction is exclusively available to Individual Taxpayers (both resident and non-resident).
Can I claim 80EEB if the EV loan was taken from a friend or relative?
No. The EV loan must be sanctioned by a financial institution, scheduled commercial bank, or registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC).
What is the maximum tax saved under Section 80EEB?
For an individual in the 30% tax bracket (+ 4% Cess = 31.2%), claiming the maximum ₹1,50,000 deduction saves ₹46,800 in income tax annually!
Is principal repayment of an EV loan tax-deductible?
No. Section 80EEB ONLY applies to the INTEREST component of the EV loan. Principal repayment does not qualify for Section 80C or 80EEB deductions.
