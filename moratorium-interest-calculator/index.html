How Does a Loan Moratorium Work?

A loan moratorium provides temporary relief from EMI payments. However, interest continues to accumulate during the pause. At the end of the moratorium, the accrued interest is added to your principal balance (capitalized), resulting in higher future EMIs.

How Loan Moratorium Interest Compounding Works

  • Not an Interest Waiver: A moratorium pauses monthly EMI payments, but interest continues to accrue and compound on the unpaid loan balance.
  • Capitalization of Interest: Accrued moratorium interest is added to your principal balance, resulting in higher monthly EMIs or extended loan tenure.

Evaluating Moratorium Costs on Education & Home Loans

  • Education Loan Moratorium: Interest accrued during course tenure + 1-year grace period can increase principal by 25-30% before repayment starts.
  • Sec 80E Relief: Interest paid on education loans is 100% tax-deductible under Section 80E for 8 consecutive years with no upper cap.

Moratorium Avoidance Advice

Whenever possible, pay simple interest monthly during an education or home loan moratorium to prevent interest capitalization from expanding your debt balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a loan moratorium?
A loan moratorium is a temporary period during which a borrower is not required to make monthly EMI payments. However, interest continues to accrue during this period.
Is moratorium interest waived by banks?
No, a moratorium is not an interest waiver. Interest accrues monthly on the outstanding loan principal and is capitalized (added to the principal balance).
How does moratorium impact education loans?
In education loans, the moratorium period spans the course duration plus 6-12 months. Simple interest accrued during this time is added to the principal before EMI repayment starts.
What options do borrowers have after moratorium ends?
Borrowers can either increase their monthly EMI to keep tenure constant or keep EMI constant and extend the loan tenure.
How is moratorium interest calculated?
Accrued Interest = Principal * ((1 + Monthly Interest Rate)^Moratorium Months - 1).
Can I pay simple interest during the moratorium?
Yes, paying simple interest during the moratorium prevents interest capitalization and reduces your future loan burden significantly.
Does moratorium affect CIBIL credit score?
Opting for an officially sanctioned bank moratorium does not negatively impact your CIBIL credit score.
Should I opt for a moratorium if offered?
If you have sufficient cash flow, it is recommended to continue paying regular EMIs to avoid accrued interest costs.
