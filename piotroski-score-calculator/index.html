Piotroski 9-Point Fundamental Score Matrix

  • Strong Value Stocks (8-9 Points): High financial health, improving profit margins, strengthening balance sheet.
  • Moderate Health (5-7 Points): Stable corporate performance; requires industry catalyst.
  • Weak / Value Trap (0-4 Points): Deteriorating cash flows, high debt, earnings dilution risk.

9 Specific Checklist Rules Across 3 Groups

  • Profitability (4 pts): Positive Net Income (+1), Positive Operating Cash Flow (+1), Higher ROA YoY (+1), CFO > Net Income (+1).
  • Leverage/Liquidity (3 pts): Lower Long-term Debt YoY (+1), Higher Current Ratio YoY (+1), Zero Share Dilution (+1).
  • Operating Efficiency (2 pts): Higher Gross Margin YoY (+1), Higher Asset Turnover YoY (+1).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Piotroski F-Score?
The Piotroski F-Score is a 9-point scoring system developed by Stanford Accounting Professor Joseph Piotroski to evaluate the financial strength of value stocks across Profitability, Leverage/Liquidity, and Operating Efficiency.
What score range indicates a strong company?
A score of 8 or 9 indicates high financial health and turnaround potential. A score of 0 to 4 indicates weak fundamentals and high financial distress risk.
What are the 4 Profitability Criteria?
1. Positive Net Income (+1), 2. Positive Operating Cash Flow (+1), 3. Higher Return on Assets (ROA) YoY (+1), 4. Cash Flow from Operations > Net Income (+1).
What are the 3 Leverage and Liquidity Criteria?
5. Lower Long-term Debt Ratio YoY (+1), 6. Higher Current Ratio YoY (+1), 7. Zero Share Dilution / No new equity shares issued (+1).
What are the 2 Operating Efficiency Criteria?
8. Higher Gross Margin percentage YoY (+1), 9. Higher Asset Turnover ratio YoY (+1).
Why is Cash Flow > Net Income important?
This rule ensures earnings quality, confirming that profits are supported by real cash inflow rather than aggressive accrual accounting.
Can Piotroski F-Score outperform market indices?
Academic studies by Joseph Piotroski showed that buying high F-Score (8-9) value stocks and shorting low F-Score (0-2) stocks generated an annual outperformance of 7.5% over benchmark indices.
Which type of stocks is Piotroski F-Score best suited for?
It is best suited for low price-to-book (P/B) value stocks, small-cap value companies, and turnaround candidates.
