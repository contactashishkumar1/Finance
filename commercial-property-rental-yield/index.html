Gross vs Net Rental Yield Formulas

  • Gross Rental Yield %: (Annual Gross Rent / Total Property Purchase Price) * 100.
  • Net Rental Yield %: [(Annual Rent - Property Tax - Maintenance - Insurance - Vacancy Loss) / Total Property Cost] * 100.

Income Tax Section 24(a) 30% Standard Deduction

  • Statutory 30% Deduction: Under Income from House Property, every property owner automatically gets a flat 30% standard deduction on Net Annual Value for repairs/maintenance regardless of actual expense incurred.
  • Cap Rate Benchmark: Prime Grade-A commercial office space in Bengaluru, Mumbai, and NCR yields 7.5% - 9.0% Cap Rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good commercial property rental yield in India?
A gross commercial rental yield of 7.0% to 9.5% per annum is considered attractive in prime Indian commercial hubs (office spaces, retail shops, warehouses).
What is the difference between Gross Rental Yield and Net Rental Yield?
Gross Yield = (Annual Rent / Purchase Price) * 100. Net Yield = ((Annual Rent - Property Tax - Maintenance - Vacancy Loss) / Total Property Investment) * 100.
What is Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)?
Cap Rate measures the net operating income (NOI) of a commercial property relative to its total asset purchase value: Cap Rate = NOI / Property Cost.
What is Section 24(a) Standard Deduction on Rental Income?
Under Income Tax Section 24(a), a statutory 30% deduction is granted on net annual rental value for repairs and maintenance without needing actual bills.
Is GST applicable on commercial property rent?
Yes, commercial property rent attracts 18% GST if the landlord's total annual turnover exceeds ₹20 Lakhs.
What is Cash-on-Cash Return?
Cash-on-Cash Return measures pre-tax annual cash flow relative to the actual out-of-pocket cash down payment invested in a leveraged property.
Why are commercial rental yields higher than residential yields in India?
Commercial properties offer longer lease lock-in periods (3 to 9 years), corporate tenants, and annual escalation clauses (5% to 15% every 3 years).
What is a Triple Net Lease (NNN)?
In a Triple Net Lease, the corporate tenant agrees to pay all property taxes, building insurance, and maintenance costs in addition to rent.
