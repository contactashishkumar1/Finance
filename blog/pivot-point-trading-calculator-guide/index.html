⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Pivot Points calculate objective, un-biased price levels based on the previous trading session's High, Low, and Close. The central Pivot Point (P) acts as the primary focal point dividing bullish and bearish market sentiment.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Central Pivot (P) = (High + Low + Close) / 3.
  • Price above Pivot P = Bullish intraday bias (Target R1, R2, R3).
  • Price below Pivot P = Bearish intraday bias (Target S1, S2, S3).
  • Camarilla Pivots (H3/L3, H4/L4): Ideal for mean-reversion and breakout trading strategies.

Central Pivot (P):(24,100 + 23,900 + 24,000) / 3 = 24,000 Level

Camarilla vs Classic vs Fibonacci Pivot Systems: Which Should You Use?

Different pivot calculation formulas serve distinct trading timeframes:

  • Standard Classic Floor Pivots: Best for swing traders and multi-day trend followers tracking major market shifts.
  • Camarilla Pivots (H3/L3 & H4/L4): The premier system for intraday mean-reversion traders. H3/L3 act as strong reversal bounce zones; breaches of H4/L4 signal aggressive intraday breakout momentum.
  • Confluence with Volume Profile: When a Daily Pivot level aligns with a major 50-period moving average or high-volume node, the probability of a sharp price reaction increases significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Pivot Point in day trading?

A Pivot Point is a technical indicator derived from the previous day's high, low, and close prices, used to identify key intraday support and resistance levels.

Why are Camarilla Pivots popular for option buyers?

Camarilla Pivots provide precise H3/L3 reversal boundary levels for range trades and clear H4/L4 breakout trigger points for explosive option buying moves.

Do Pivot Points change during trading hours?

No! Daily Pivot Points remain static throughout the entire 6.5-hour trading day because they are calculated from yesterday's completed daily candle.

What happens when price opens directly at R1?

An open above R1 indicates extreme bullish sentiment. Traders look for a minor dip to R1 (now acting as support) before buying.

Can Weekly and Monthly Pivots be plotted on daily charts?

Yes! Weekly and Monthly Pivots represent major institutional support and resistance zones for positional option traders.
