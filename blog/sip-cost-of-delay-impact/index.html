SIP Cost of Delay: How Delaying 5 Years Cuts Your Retirement Corpus in Half

Procrastination is the single greatest destroyer of compounding. Learn how starting your SIP 5 to 10 years earlier creates massive wealth with significantly less effort.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Compounding gains accelerate in the final years of an investment horizon. Delaying a 25-year SIP by just 5 years can reduce your final maturity corpus by 45% to 50%.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Time in the market matters far more than timing the market.
  • The final 5 years of a 20-year SIP generate over 50% of total lifetime wealth.
  • Catching up on a 5-year delay requires doubling your monthly SIP amount.
  • Start small immediately rather than waiting for a higher salary.

How to Recover from a Delayed Investment Start

If you did not start investing in your early 20s, you can still catch up using these mathematical strategies:

  • Aggressive Annual Step-Ups: Stepping up your monthly SIP by 15% or 20% annually (instead of standard 10%) can compress a 20-year wealth goal into 12 to 14 years.
  • Deploying Windfalls & Bonuses: Channeling 70% of annual performance bonuses and tax refunds into lump sum top-ups creates an immediate capital boost that compensates for lost early years.
  • Maintaining Higher Equity Allocation: Keep low-yielding fixed deposits limited strictly to your 6-month emergency fund, allowing the bulk of your surplus to compound in high-growth equity index funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of delay in SIP?

It is the loss of potential compound growth caused by postponing your investment start date by months or years.

Can I make up for a 5-year delay later?

Yes, but you must invest roughly 80% to 100% higher monthly SIP amounts to reach the exact same target corpus.

Is ₹1,000 a month worth starting a SIP?

Absolutely! A ₹1,000 monthly SIP started at age 22 at 12% return grows to ₹35+ Lakh by age 52.

How does step-up SIP combat delay?

Increasing your SIP by 10% annually accelerates wealth creation, helping compensate for lost time.

Should I wait for a market crash to start SIP?

No. Market timing attempts usually lead to further delay. Monthly SIPs automatically average out market highs and lows.
