Stamp Duty & Property Registration Fees Across Indian States: Rates & Savings

Buying a house? Stamp duty and registration charges add 5% to 9% extra cash out-of-pocket beyond the property price. Learn how state rates and concessions work.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Stamp duty is a state tax levied on property transactions calculated on market agreement value or Circle Rate/Ready Reckoner rate (whichever is higher). Registration fee is typically 1% of property value.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Stamp duty rates range from 4% to 8% depending on the state and municipal area.
  • Women buyers enjoy 1% to 2% lower stamp duty rates in states like Delhi, UP, and Haryana.
  • Property registration fee is generally 1% of agreement value (capped at ₹30,000 in some states).
  • Stamp duty paid can be claimed as a tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 Lakh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Circle Rate / Ready Reckoner Rate?

Circle Rate is the minimum threshold price per sq ft set by state governments below which property cannot be registered.

Can stamp duty be paid via home loan?

Most banks do not include stamp duty and registration fees in the home loan LTV sanction amount. You must pay it from personal savings.

Is stamp duty tax deductible?

Yes, stamp duty and registration charges paid for residential house purchase qualify for tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 Lakh.

What happens if I register property below Circle Rate?

Under Section 56(2)(x), registering below circle rate triggers double taxation on both buyer and seller for the price difference.

How is joint ownership stamp duty calculated?

If a property is jointly registered with a woman co-owner, many states grant partial or full 1% stamp duty concessions.
