Frequently Asked Questions

What is subscription leakage?

Subscription leakage is the cumulative financial loss from small recurring auto-payments for services you no longer use or value.

How do I find all auto-debits on my bank account?

Check your credit card statement 'Recurring Charges' section, check UPI e-mandates on Paytm/PhonePe/GPay, and review Net Banking standing instructions.

What is RBI's e-mandate rule for recurring payments?

RBI mandates that banks send an AFA (Additional Factor of Authentication) SMS notification 24 hours before any recurring auto-debit takes place.

How do I cancel a recurring UPI mandate?

Open GPay/PhonePe/Paytm -> Go to Settings -> Autopay / UPI Mandates -> Select Mandate -> Tap 'Pause' or 'Revoke Mandate'.

What is the 30-day manual renewal rule?

Never enable auto-renew on annual subscriptions. Always opt for manual renewal so you actively decide whether the service is worth paying for again.
