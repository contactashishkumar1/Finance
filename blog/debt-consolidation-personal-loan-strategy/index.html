Debt Consolidation Strategy: Merging Multiple Loans into a Single Lower EMI

Juggling 4 different EMIs across credit cards, personal loans, and BNPL apps? Learn how debt consolidation simplifies your finances into one low-interest EMI.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Debt consolidation combines multiple high-interest debts (credit cards at 42%, BNPL at 28%) into a single, structured personal loan at a much lower interest rate (11%-14%).

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Replaces multiple chaotic EMI dates with 1 single monthly payment date.
  • Drops average interest rate from 30%+ down to 12%-14%.
  • Protects credit score by preventing missed payment defaults.
  • Stops the psychological stress of managing multiple debt collectors.

Debt Avalanche vs Debt Snowball: Choosing Your Repayment Framework

Once you consolidate or organize your debts, choose a structured mathematical framework to clear outstanding balances:

  • Debt Avalanche (Mathematically Optimal): Order debts from highest interest rate to lowest interest rate (e.g. Credit Card at 42% > Personal Loan at 14% > Car Loan at 9% > Home Loan at 8.5%). Pay minimums on all and direct 100% of surplus cash to eliminate the highest-rate debt first.
  • Debt Snowball (Psychological Momentum): Order debts from smallest balance to largest balance regardless of rate. Clearing small balances quickly provides psychological wins that build behavioral momentum.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Debt Consolidation?

Debt consolidation is the process of taking out one new loan to pay off several existing high-interest debts, leaving you with just one monthly payment.

Does debt consolidation eliminate my debt?

No. It does not erase your debt balance, but it significantly reduces the interest rate and structures your payments into a fixed payoff timeline.

What is the snowball vs avalanche debt method?

Debt Avalanche pays off highest-interest debt first to minimize cost. Debt Snowball pays off smallest balances first to build psychological momentum.

Will I qualify for a consolidation personal loan with low CIBIL?

If your CIBIL score is below 650, you may need a co-applicant or a loan against property / mutual funds to secure a consolidation loan.

What is the golden rule after consolidating debt?

Close or stop using the credit cards you paid off; otherwise you risk accumulating new debt on top of your consolidation loan.
