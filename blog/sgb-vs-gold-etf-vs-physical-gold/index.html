Gold ETF vs Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) vs Physical Gold: Returns, Tax & Yield

Gold remains India's favorite asset class for wealth preservation. Compare sovereign gold bonds, gold ETFs, and physical gold to maximize yields and eliminate making charges.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) offer 2.5% fixed annual interest plus 100% tax-free capital gains on maturity. Gold ETFs offer superior liquidity for short-term trading but carry expense ratios.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • SGBs pay 2.5% annual interest on initial investment in addition to gold price appreciation.
  • SGB capital gains are 100% tax-free if held until maturity (8 years).
  • Physical gold jewellery suffers from 10%-25% making charge losses plus 3% GST.
  • Gold ETFs provide instant liquidity on stock exchanges without storage risk.

Buying SGBs from the Secondary Market (NSE/BSE) at a Discount

While RBI has slowed down fresh primary issuances of Sovereign Gold Bonds, smart investors purchase existing SGB tranches from the secondary stock market:

  • Secondary Market Price Discounts: Due to low liquidity on stock exchanges, many SGB tranches trade at a 2% to 5% discount to the spot gold price. Buying at a discount boosts your effective annual Yield-to-Maturity.
  • Tax Exemption Rule on Secondary Purchases: Under Section 47 of the Income Tax Act, capital gains on redemption of SGBs with the RBI upon final 8-year maturity are 100% tax-free for individual investors, even if the bond was purchased in the secondary market!
  • Semi-Annual 2.5% Sovereign Interest: You continue to receive 2.5% p.a. simple interest on the initial issue price, credited directly to your bank account every 6 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Sovereign Gold Bonds better than Gold ETFs?

Yes, for long-term investors (5-8 years), SGBs are superior because of the 2.5% annual interest payout and 100% tax exemption on maturity.

Is SGB interest taxable?

Yes, the 2.5% annual interest paid by the RBI is added to your income and taxed at your applicable income tax slab rate.

Can I sell SGB before 8 years?

Yes, SGBs are traded on stock exchanges (NSE/BSE) and have an official exit window with RBI after 5 years on interest payment dates.

What are the making charges on gold jewellery?

Making charges range from 8% to 25% depending on design complexity, representing an immediate unrecoverable loss upon purchase.

What is Digital Gold?

Digital gold allows buying fractional gold online, but it attracts 3% GST, storage fee caps, and lacks RBI sovereign backing.
