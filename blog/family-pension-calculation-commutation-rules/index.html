Central & State Govt Family Pension Calculation: Commutation & Restoration Rules

Retiring from government service or calculating family pension entitlements? Learn how pension commutation, 15-year restoration, and family pension rules work.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Govt employees can commute (sell) up to 40% of their basic pension for an upfront tax-free lump sum. Reduced monthly pension continues for 15 years, after which the full pension is automatically restored.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Commutation Cap: Central Govt employees can commute up to 40% of basic pension.
  • Tax Exemption: 100% of commuted pension lump sum is tax-free for Govt employees.
  • Restoration Period: Commuted portion is restored automatically after 15 years.
  • Family Pension Rate: 30% of last basic pay (or 50% enhanced rate for initial 7 years).

How to compute Commutation Lump Sum = 40% Basic Pension × 12 × Commutation Factor Value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is pension commutation?

Commutation is the process where a retiree foregoes a portion (up to 40%) of their monthly pension in exchange for an immediate tax-free lump sum payout.

When does commuted pension get restored?

The commuted portion of basic pension is automatically restored after 15 years from the date of commutation payment.

Is commuted pension tax-free for private sector employees?

For non-govt employees receiving gratuity, 1/3rd of pension is tax-exempt. If no gratuity is received, 1/2 of pension is tax-exempt.

What is Enhanced Family Pension?

If a employee dies in service or after retirement, the family receives 50% of last basic pay for 7 years or until age 67, whichever is earlier.

What is the tax deduction for family pension under New Tax Regime?

Under the New Tax Regime (Section 57(iia)), family pension receives a standard deduction of ₹25,000 (or 33.33% of pension, whichever is lower).
