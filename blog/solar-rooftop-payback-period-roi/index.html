PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Subsidy Structure & Net Metering

Under the Central Government's PM Surya Ghar scheme, residential homeowners receive upfront capital subsidies of ₹30,000 for 1kW systems, ₹60,000 for 2kW systems, and up to ₹78,000 for 3kW or larger rooftop solar installations.

Net metering allows your solar inverter to export excess daytime electricity generation directly back into the state DISCOM grid. On monthly electricity bills, exported units are offset against imported nighttime units, reducing monthly power bills down to minimal fixed grid charges.

25-Year Panel Degradation vs Inverter Replacement Costs

Tier-1 Mono PERC solar panels carry 25-year performance warranties with an annual power output degradation of ~0.55%. However, string solar inverters typically require replacement between Year 8 and Year 12, costing approximately ₹25,000 to ₹40,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

It is a central government scheme providing up to ₹78,000 direct subsidy to install rooftop solar systems for up to 300 units of free electricity monthly.

How does Net Metering work?

Net metering is a bi-directional electricity meter that measures electricity drawn from the grid versus solar power fed into the grid, billing you only for net consumption.

How long do rooftop solar panels last?

Tier-1 solar panels carry a 25-year linear performance warranty promising at least 80% generation capacity after 25 years.

Do solar panels work during rain or winter?

Yes, solar panels generate power during cloudy days using indirect sunlight, producing about 25%-40% of their peak capacity.

Do solar panels work during a power outage?

On-grid solar systems automatically shut off during a power grid outage for safety (anti-islanding). Off-grid systems with batteries continue providing backup.
