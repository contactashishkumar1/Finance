Quick Answer & Overview

Fibonacci Retracement uses key mathematical ratios (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%) derived from the Golden Ratio to identify horizontal support and resistance levels during market pullbacks.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Golden Ratio (61.8%): The most critical Fibonacci reversal zone in swing trading.
  • 38.2% & 50% Levels: Indicate a healthy, strong ongoing trend pullback.
  • 78.6% Level: Last line of defense before a trend completely reverses.
  • Extensions (161.8%, 261.8%): Used to project profit-taking targets in breakout trades.

The Mathematical Foundation of the 61.8% Golden Ratio in Market Swings

Fibonacci Retracements in financial markets are derived from the mathematical Golden Ratio (φ ≈ 1.618033):

  • The 61.8% Golden Retracement: Dividing any number in the Fibonacci sequence by its succeeding number (e.g. 55 / 89) yields 0.618. In stock and index charts, the 61.8% level represents the premier pullback support zone during healthy bull market trends.
  • Fibonacci Extension Targets (161.8% & 261.8%): Once a stock breaks out above its prior swing high, the 1.618 and 2.618 extensions serve as high-probability profit-taking targets for swing traders.
  • Confluence Trading: Never enter a trade based on a Fibonacci line alone. Look for confluence with candlestick reversal patterns (hammer, engulfing) and RSI divergence at the 61.8% level.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fibonacci Retracement in technical analysis?

It is a technical analysis tool that uses horizontal lines to indicate where potential support and resistance areas are likely to occur based on Golden Ratios.

Why is 61.8% called the Golden Ratio?

The ratio 0.618 is derived by dividing any Fibonacci number by its succeeding number (e.g., 55/89 = 0.6179), reflecting natural growth proportions found across nature and financial markets.

How do I draw Fibonacci Retracement on an uptrend?

Click on the major Swing Low first and drag the cursor up to the major Swing High.

What is the difference between Fibonacci Retracement and Extension?

Retracement measures internal pullbacks within an existing range, while Extension projects target price levels beyond the previous swing high.

Can Fibonacci levels be used for intraday trading?

Yes! Fibonacci levels work effectively on 5-minute, 15-minute, 1-hour, and daily chart timeframes.
