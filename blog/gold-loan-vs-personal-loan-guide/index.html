Gold Loan vs Personal Loan: Interest Rates, LTV Ratios & Processing Fees

Need quick funds for a medical emergency or business need? Compare secured Gold Loans against unsecured Personal Loans to minimize borrowing costs.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Gold Loans are secured against gold ornaments, offering lower interest rates (8%-10%) and instant approval with minimal credit score requirements. Personal Loans are unsecured, carrying higher rates (12%-18%) and strict CIBIL criteria.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Gold loans offer interest rates 3% to 6% lower than personal loans.
  • RBI caps Gold Loan Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio at 75% of gold market value.
  • Personal loans require a CIBIL score of 750+ and proof of stable income.
  • Gold loan bullet repayment options allow paying interest monthly and principal at maturity.

LTV (Loan to Value) Ratios and Gold Price Volatility Risk

When taking a Gold Loan from commercial banks or NBFCs (Muthoot, Manappuram), understand RBI regulatory constraints:

  • 75% LTV Regulatory Cap: RBI mandates that banks can lend a maximum of 75% of the market value of the gold jewelry (excluding stone weight and making charges).
  • Margin Call Risk on Price Drops: If global gold prices drop sharply, your LTV may exceed the 75% threshold. The lender will issue a margin call requiring you to either pledge additional gold or pay down part of the cash balance within 7 days to avoid auction of your pledged ornaments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum loan amount on gold?

RBI rules cap gold loans at 75% of the assessed market value of the gold (excluding making charges and gemstones).

What happens if gold prices drop during loan tenure?

If gold prices drop sharply, banks may request additional margin/collateral or partial principal repayment to maintain the 75% LTV cap.

Is my gold safe with NBFCs/Banks?

Regulated banks and NBFCs store pledged gold in fireproof, insured vault safes with 24/7 security.

What is bullet repayment in a gold loan?

Bullet repayment allows you to pay only monthly interest during the tenure, repaying the entire principal in one single lump sum at loan maturity.

Which is better for a self-employed person with low CIBIL?

A Gold Loan is far easier to secure because approval depends on collateral value rather than income proof or credit history.
