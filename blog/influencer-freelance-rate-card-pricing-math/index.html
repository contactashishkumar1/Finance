Freelancer Hourly Rate & Campaign Pricing Math: Calculating Sustainable Fees

Transitioning to full-time freelancing or content creation? Learn how to set sustainable hourly billing rates and campaign pricing to avoid undercharging.

⚡ Executive Summary

Quick Answer & Overview

Freelance rate pricing calculates your required hourly rate by combining your target net annual income, business overheads, unpaid admin/prospecting hours, and vacation time.

📌 Key Takeaways

  • Billable Hours Trap: Freelancers only bill 50% to 60% of total working hours (rest is prospecting/admin).
  • Target Revenue = Target Income + Software Overheads + Healthcare + Retirement Savings + Taxes.
  • Hourly Rate = Total Target Revenue / Total Billable Hours.
  • Project/Value-Based Pricing generates 2x-3x higher returns than simple hourly billing.

🎯 What You'll Learn

The Billable Hours Equation: 2,000 annual working hours -> 1,000 actual billable hours.

Always model cash flows on a net post-tax, post-fee basis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why shouldn't I divide my target salary by 2,000 hours?

Dividing by 2,000 hours assumes 100% of your time is spent on paid client work. In reality, 40% of time goes into non-billable tasks (invoicing, emails, sales, marketing).

What is Value-Based Pricing?

Value-based pricing charges clients based on the financial outcome/value created for their business rather than the number of hours worked.

How do I quote for brand sponsorship campaigns?

Factor in your reach, engagement rate, production cost, usage rights duration, and exclusivity restrictions.

Should I charge advance deposit payments?

Always collect a 30% to 50% upfront non-refundable deposit before beginning any freelance project.

How do I account for paid time off as a freelancer?

Include 3 to 4 weeks of paid vacation and sick leave in your annual billable hours calculation so your rate covers time off.
