If car loan interest rates are 8.5% to 9.0% and long-term equity SIP returns average 12% to 14%, investing surplus cash in SIPs while taking a car loan often generates higher net wealth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to pay cash for a car or take a car loan in India?

If you can invest surplus cash in equity mutual funds generating 12%+ CAGR while securing a car loan at 8.5%, financing the car and investing surplus cash builds higher net worth.

What is the golden rule for buying a car (20/4/10 Rule)?

Pay at least 20% down payment, limit loan tenure to 4 years, and ensure total car expenses (EMI + fuel + insurance) consume less than 10% of gross monthly income.

Do car loans carry prepayment penalties?

Most floating-rate car loans carry zero prepayment penalty, but fixed-rate car loans charge 2% to 5% prepayment fees.

Why shouldn't I dump all my emergency savings into a car down payment?

Cars are non-liquid depreciating assets. Exhausting your emergency cash reserves leaves you vulnerable to sudden financial shocks.

What is the typical annual depreciation rate for a new car?

New cars lose approx 15% to 20% value in Year 1, and 10% annually thereafter.
